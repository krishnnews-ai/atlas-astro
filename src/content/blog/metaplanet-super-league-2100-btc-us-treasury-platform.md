Metaplanet has moved a step closer to establishing a Bitcoin treasury platform on U.S. soil. On October 16, 2026, shareholders of Nasdaq-listed Super League Enterprise, Inc. (Nasdaq: SLE) approved all proposals related to Metaplanet's proposed strategic investment, according to an official company announcement published on October 19.

The approval marks a significant milestone in Metaplanet's evolution from a Tokyo-listed Bitcoin treasury company into what its leadership describes as a global Bitcoin-native financial platform. The deal, first announced in August 2026, will see Metaplanet contribute 2,100 Bitcoin and $2.5 million in cash to Super League at closing.

## What Exactly Was Approved?

Super League stockholders voted in favor of all proposals at the company's 2026 Annual Meeting, including the share issuance, charter amendments, and governance changes required to complete the transaction.

Under the definitive agreement, Metaplanet—through its wholly owned U.S. subsidiary, Metaplanet Holdings, Inc.—will transfer 2,100 BTC and $2.5 million in cash to Super League. In exchange, Metaplanet will receive 44,859,400 shares of common stock at $3.00 per share, along with 100 shares of convertible perpetual preferred stock and ten-year warrants to purchase up to 381 million additional shares.

The Bitcoin was valued at approximately $132.1 million based on the Coinbase closing price on August 14, 2026—the reference date under the agreement. By October 16, 2026, that same 2,100 BTC was worth approximately $170 million, reflecting Bitcoin's price appreciation during the period.

## Why This Deal Matters Beyond the Numbers

The headline figures are impressive, but the strategic logic runs deeper. At closing, Super League will be renamed **Superplanet, Inc.** and will trade on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol **SUPA**. It will serve as Metaplanet's U.S.-listed Bitcoin treasury platform, operating alongside the company's existing Tokyo Stock Exchange listing.

Metaplanet CEO Simon Gerovich framed the approval as a pivotal moment. "This approval will provide the company with complementary platforms in the two most important capital markets globally—the United States and Japan," he said, according to reporting from ChainCatcher.

The structure allows Metaplanet to access U.S. capital markets for future fundraising while maintaining its Japanese base. Gerovich described it succinctly: "Japan gave us our foundation. America gives us a second engine."

## The Mechanics: Lock-Up, Warrants, and Future Capital

One of the most notable aspects of the deal is the **five-year lock-up** Metaplanet has agreed to for all shares issued at closing, as well as any shares acquired through warrants or preferred stock conversion. This is not a short-term trade. It signals a multi-year commitment.

The warrants structure is also significant. The ten-year warrants are divided into four tranches with exercise prices stepping up from $3.00 to $33.50 per share. If fully exercised, they could provide Superplanet with up to approximately $3.4 billion in additional equity capital.

Additionally, for 24 months after closing, Metaplanet has the right to invest up to an additional $210 million through non-convertible junior liquidity preferred stock.

## What Happens to Super League's Existing Business?

Super League is not being dismantled. Its advertising and media activation business—which connects brands with the global gaming audience—will continue as a distinct operating segment. Matt Edelman will remain Chief Executive Officer.

The company's board will be restructured, with Metaplanet designating the chairman and holding five of nine board seats, according to CoinMarketCap's reporting on the deal structure.

In a statement, Edelman said: "If the transaction closes, we would no longer be defined solely by our current scale. Alongside an operating business that continues to serve many of the world's most recognizable brands, we would have a treasury seeded with 2,100 Bitcoin and the long-term backing of one of the largest corporate Bitcoin holders globally."

## What's Next?

The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to customary closing conditions, including required filings with the Nasdaq Capital Market.

When it closes, Metaplanet will hold approximately 93.7% of Superplanet's common stock, according to the company's announcement. This gives Metaplanet effective control while leaving a portion of shares in public hands.

For the broader crypto market, this deal represents more than a corporate transaction. It is a template for how Bitcoin treasury companies can expand beyond their home markets, access new pools of capital, and build multi-jurisdictional structures—all without selling their Bitcoin.
