What would make a successful businessman donate 121 kilograms of gold to a temple? For Chennai-based entrepreneur Vardhaman Jain, the answer lies in faith, gratitude and a promise he wanted to fulfil.

On November 26, 2026, Jain, accompanied by his wife Radhika Jain, presented an extraordinary collection of gold jewellery and precious stones to Lord Venkateswara at Tirumala in Andhra Pradesh.

The offering, valued at approximately **₹2,07,60,00,000 (₹207.6 crore)**, was made in the presence of Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu.

---

## Why Did He Choose Exactly 121 KG of Gold?

The most interesting part of this story is how the donation began.

Jain, who built a successful healthcare business with his brother Anurag Jain, reportedly decided to offer 121 kilograms of gold to Lord Venkateswara after a major business milestone.

He initially approached temple authorities in August 2025 with the intention of donating raw gold.

However, after discussions with Chief Minister Chandrababu Naidu, the plan changed. Instead of raw gold, Jain agreed to have specially designed ornaments prepared for the deity.

The entire process reportedly took around 15 months.

---

## 46 Gold Ornaments and Hundreds of Carats of Diamonds

This was no ordinary gold donation.

The collection includes 46 specially crafted ornaments arranged in nine sets, featuring gold, diamonds, rubies, emeralds and other precious stones.

Here's what makes the offering special:

- **106 KG of gold:** Used for ornaments dedicated to Lord Venkateswara.
- **15 KG of gold:** Allocated for a golden garment for Goddess Padmavathi.
- **238.2 carats of diamonds:** Featured in the jewellery collection.
- **46 ornaments:** Including crowns, necklaces, bracelets and traditional temple jewellery.

## A Gift Rooted in Faith

The donation ceremony was attended by temple officials, priests and government representatives.

Tirumala is among the world's most visited pilgrimage destinations, where devotees have offered gold, jewellery and other valuable possessions for centuries.

For Vardhaman Jain and his family, this extraordinary offering represents more than its financial value. It is a personal expression of devotion and a promise fulfilled.

**A businessman, 121 kilograms of gold and a donation worth ₹207.6 crore — this is a remarkable reminder of how deeply faith can shape a person's biggest decisions.**
