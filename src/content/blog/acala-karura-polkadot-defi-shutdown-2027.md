The Polkadot ecosystem is facing another major setback. Acala and its Kusama-based sister network Karura are planning a permanent shutdown on February 10, 2027, bringing an end to two projects that once stood among the most important developments in the ecosystem.

According to Wu Blockchain, the proposed shutdown still requires approval through Acala Referendum #195 and Karura Referendum #137.

The announcement is particularly significant because Acala and Karura were the first winners of the parachain slot auctions on Polkadot and Kusama, respectively. Their launch represented an important milestone in the expansion of interconnected blockchain networks.

---

## Why Are Acala and Karura Shutting Down?

The central issue is liquidity.

The teams have cited insufficient and fragmented decentralized finance (DeFi) liquidity across the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems as the primary reason for winding down the networks.

In simple terms, DeFi platforms need enough liquidity to support trading, lending, borrowing and other financial activities. Without sufficient capital and active users, maintaining a competitive decentralized finance ecosystem becomes increasingly difficult.

The decision highlights a broader challenge facing blockchain projects: launching a network is only the beginning. Building sustainable liquidity, attracting developers and retaining users are equally important for long-term survival.

---

## February 10, 2027: The Date Users Need to Watch

Users are being urged to withdraw their external assets before the proposed shutdown date.

Assets specifically highlighted include:

- **DOT:** Polkadot's native token.
- **KSM:** Kusama's native token.
- **USDT:** Tether's dollar-pegged stablecoin.
- **USDC:** Circle's dollar-pegged stablecoin.

However, there is an important distinction. According to the reported shutdown plan, native ACA and KAR tokens cannot be withdrawn.

This means holders should not assume that every asset on these networks will have the same withdrawal options.

Users should review the official governance proposals and available withdrawal instructions before making transactions. They should also verify the correct destination network and wallet addresses to avoid losing funds.

---

## Two Projects That Once Led the Polkadot Ecosystem

Acala was designed as a decentralized finance hub for Polkadot, while Karura served as its experimental counterpart on Kusama.

Both networks were part of the early vision of the parachain ecosystem, where specialized blockchains could operate alongside a shared underlying network.

Their initial auction victories attracted considerable attention and helped establish the early DeFi infrastructure around Polkadot.

Their planned closure now raises questions about the long-term sustainability of smaller blockchain ecosystems, particularly those struggling to attract enough liquidity and user activity.

---

## What Happens Next?

The proposed February 10, 2027 shutdown is not yet unconditional. Both governance votes must receive approval before the plan can proceed.

For now, users should monitor Acala Referendum #195 and Karura Referendum #137, follow official project communications and prioritize moving eligible external assets ahead of any approved shutdown.

**The bigger message for crypto is difficult to ignore: Winning a blockchain auction can create an opportunity, but it does not guarantee a sustainable DeFi ecosystem. Acala and Karura's proposed exit could become an important lesson for the next generation of parachain projects.**

---

## The Bottom Line

Acala and Karura — the first parachain auction winners on Polkadot and Kusama — are preparing to shut down permanently on February 10, 2027, pending governance approval.

The reason: insufficient and fragmented DeFi liquidity across both ecosystems.

Users need to withdraw DOT, KSM, USDT and USDC before the deadline. Native ACA and KAR tokens cannot be withdrawn.

The bigger lesson: Winning a parachain auction is not enough. Sustainable liquidity, active users and developer adoption are what keep a DeFi ecosystem alive.
