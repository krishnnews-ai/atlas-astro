Nvidia has delivered a dramatic surprise to Wall Street. After suffering a brutal 14% decline in its biggest single-day drop in more than a year, the AI chipmaker suddenly bounced back in premarket trading on Monday, November 23.

Shares of Nvidia (NASDAQ: NVDA) climbed around 8.8%, moving above $136, according to figures shared by Coin Bureau. The sudden recovery came after the stock had fallen to its lowest level in more than a year.

If the rally holds during regular trading, Nvidia could add approximately $260 billion to its market capitalization, potentially taking its valuation towards $3.3 trillion.

For investors who watched Friday's sell-off, the reversal is remarkable. But the big question is whether this is the beginning of a genuine recovery or simply a temporary bounce.

---

## Friday's 14% Crash Sent Shockwaves Through Wall Street

Friday was a painful day for Nvidia shareholders.

The stock dropped approximately 14%, wiping out a huge amount of market value in a single session. The decline also put pressure on other major semiconductor companies and technology stocks.

Nvidia has become one of the most closely watched companies in the global stock market because of its dominant position in AI chips. When its shares fall sharply, the impact often spreads across the wider technology sector.

The sell-off raised fresh questions about investor confidence in AI-related stocks and whether their valuations had become too stretched.

---

## Why Did Nvidia Suddenly Bounce Back?

After the dramatic decline, traders returned to Nvidia shares during Monday's premarket session.

The rebound came amid renewed attention on the company's leadership situation and the future of its AI business.

Nvidia's CEO Jensen Huang has been at the centre of the company's rise into one of the world's most valuable technology businesses. Any uncertainty surrounding his position can attract enormous attention from investors.

However, claims circulating online about Huang resigning should be treated carefully. There has been no verified confirmation in the supplied Coin Bureau post that he resigned.

Investors should also remember that a premarket rally does not necessarily mean the stock will maintain those gains once regular trading begins.

---

## Could Nvidia Regain $260 Billion in Market Value?

This is the figure attracting the most attention.

According to Coin Bureau's estimate, if Nvidia shares opened above $136 and maintained that level, the company's market capitalization could approach $3.3 trillion.

That would represent an increase of approximately $260 billion from its post-crash valuation.

But this is a projected increase based on the premarket share price, not a confirmed gain. The actual market value depends on the stock's final trading price.

---

## Nvidia's AI Dominance Is Still a Major Factor

Despite the volatility, Nvidia remains a central player in the artificial intelligence industry.

Its GPUs power AI training and inference systems used by major technology companies, cloud providers and research organizations.

Demand for computing power has made Nvidia one of the biggest beneficiaries of the global AI investment boom.

However, investors are increasingly paying attention to how much companies are spending on AI infrastructure and whether those investments will generate enough revenue to justify current valuations.

That uncertainty can create sharp price movements, even when a company's long-term business position remains strong.

---

## Is Nvidia's Recovery Finally Starting?

Monday's surge offers some relief after Friday's brutal decline, but one strong session does not guarantee a lasting turnaround.

Nvidia still faces questions about market valuations, competition, AI spending and investor confidence.

For traders, the immediate focus is whether NVDA can hold its recovery during regular market hours. For long-term investors, the bigger question is whether demand for AI computing will remain strong enough to support the company's enormous valuation.

One thing is clear: Nvidia's latest price swing shows just how quickly sentiment can change in the AI stock market.

## The Bottom Line

Nvidia shares surged 8.8% in premarket trading after a brutal 14% crash on Friday.

If the rally holds, the company could add approximately $260 billion to its market cap, pushing its valuation toward $3.3 trillion.

However, a premarket rally does not guarantee a lasting recovery. Nvidia still faces questions about AI spending, valuations, and leadership uncertainty.

One thing is certain: Nvidia's wild price swings show just how fragile investor sentiment has become in the AI stock market.
