**I've been watching altcoins for years, and I'll be honest: most rallies like this fade within weeks. But NEAR's recent move feels different. Here's what's actually happening.**

***

Look, I get it. Every other week some altcoin pumps 100% and everyone screams "altseason." Most of the time, it's just leverage and hype. But when I saw NEAR flip XLM on the market cap rankings this week, I had to dig deeper.

And what I found actually made me pause.

## The Numbers First

NEAR is up **138% in 30 days**. It went from around **$1.60 in early August\*\* to \*\*$5.50 as I'm writing this** on October 8, 2026. That's not a small move. That's a statement.

Market cap? Roughly **$7.2 billion\*\*. XLM? Sitting at about \*\*$6.97 billion**. So yeah, NEAR is now in the top 20. XLM got pushed down to 21st.

Crypto Rover tweeted about this exact move, and honestly, he's not wrong to highlight it.

But here's the thing — _why_ is this happening? Let me break it down without the usual crypto Twitter hype.

## Reason #1: There's Now a Spot ETF (And It's Actually Buying)

On **September 29, 2026**, Bitwise launched **NRR** — the first US spot NEAR ETF. It trades on NYSE Arca.

Now, I've seen a lot of "ETF news" that turns out to be futures-based or some gray-area product. This one is different. It actually buys and holds NEAR tokens.

Day one numbers:

- **$35.5 million** in net inflows
- **7.2 million NEAR** purchased (that's 0.55% of circulating supply in ONE day)
- **$36 million** AUM right out of the gate

Think about that for a second. Every dollar that comes into this ETF forces someone to buy NEAR on the open market. That's real buying pressure. Not imaginary.

And Bitwise? They're staking a big chunk of those holdings. So those tokens aren't just sitting there — they're earning yield while being locked up.

## Reason #2: NEAR Intents Just Crossed $31 Billion

While everyone was watching the ETF headlines, something else happened quietly.

**NEAR Intents** — the protocol's cross-chain transaction layer — crossed **$31 billion in cumulative volume**.

If you don't know what NEAR Intents does, here's the simple version: instead of you manually bridging assets across chains, paying gas on multiple networks, and dealing with slippage, you just declare what you want. The system figures out the best route and executes it.

It sounds boring. It's actually massive. Because it means NEAR isn't just another L1 competing for TVL — it's becoming infrastructure that other chains _need_.

## Why XLM Is Losing This Battle

I don't want to dunk on Stellar. It's a solid project with real use cases.

But right now? XLM is bleeding.

It dropped below **$0.20** and fell about **6.27% on October 7 alone**. Rising US Treasury yields and a stronger dollar are pulling capital away from risk assets like XLM. The futures market saw a wave of liquidations too.

It's not that XLM is broken. It's that NEAR has a _catalyst_ right now, and XLM doesn't. In crypto, narrative and momentum matter more than fundamentals in the short term. That's just reality.

## The One Thing That Worried Me

On **October 1, 2026**, NEAR Intents got exploited. About **$3.8 million** was stolen from the Omni deposit/withdrawal infrastructure on BNB Chain.

I won't lie — when I saw that headline, I raised an eyebrow.

But here's how it played out: The team patched the vulnerability in about an hour. Then they tracked the hacker, gave a 48-hour ultimatum, and — get this — **the hacker returned all $3.8 million**.

Illia Polosukhin (NEAR co-founder) confirmed the incident was isolated to a specific contract and didn't touch the NEAR blockchain or the token itself.

A hack that gets fully recovered within a week? That's not a red flag. That's actually a green flag about the team's competence.

## What I'm Watching Next

Two things:

1. **The governance proposal** to cut NEAR's annual issuance from 2.5% to 1.6%. If that passes, it's a deflationary shift that could push prices higher.
2. **The $5.50-$6.20 resistance zone.** If NEAR holds above this, I think $8 and then $12 become realistic targets. If it loses $4.70, we could see a deeper pullback.

## My Honest Take

I've been skeptical of most altcoin rallies this year. Too much leverage, too little substance.

But NEAR has:

- A spot ETF that's actually buying
- Real cross-chain volume ($31B+)
- A team that handled a hack better than most
- And a narrative (AI + intents) that fits where the market is heading

The flip of XLM isn't just a ranking change. It's a signal.

**Altseason might actually be here this time. And NEAR is leading the charge.**
