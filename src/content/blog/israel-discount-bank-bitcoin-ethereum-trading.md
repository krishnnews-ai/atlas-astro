**Something important has changed in Israel's cryptocurrency market.**

Customers of Israel Discount Bank can now access Bitcoin and Ethereum trading directly through the bank's mobile application, bringing digital assets into the same banking environment where customers already manage their traditional investments.

The development marks a significant step for cryptocurrency adoption in Israel. Instead of opening an account with an external crypto exchange, eligible customers can buy, hold and sell Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH) through their existing banking relationship.

The service was announced on November 11, with November 26 set as its launch date. The bank's official announcement confirms the new offering.

---

## Bitcoin and Ethereum Are Now Available Through a Banking App

The biggest difference is where customers can access their cryptocurrency.

Until now, people wanting to trade Bitcoin generally needed to use a dedicated cryptocurrency exchange or another digital asset service.

Discount Bank is changing that experience by bringing crypto trading into its existing mobile banking ecosystem.

Customers will be able to:

- Buy Bitcoin directly through the banking app.
- Purchase and hold Ethereum.
- Sell their cryptocurrency through the same banking platform.
- Manage crypto alongside traditional investments such as stocks and bonds.
- Receive the proceeds from crypto sales directly into their bank accounts.

Another important feature is that customers do not need to open a separate account with an overseas cryptocurrency exchange or personally manage a dedicated digital wallet.

This could make cryptocurrency more accessible to people who are comfortable using traditional banking services but have never used a crypto exchange.

---

## Fireblocks Will Power the Crypto Infrastructure

Behind the new service is Fireblocks, a financial technology company specialising in digital asset infrastructure and custody solutions.

The arrangement allows the bank to provide cryptocurrency services without requiring customers to independently manage complicated wallet infrastructure.

Customer assets will be held using bank-provided wallets supported by Fireblocks technology.

This is an important distinction: customers gain access to cryptocurrency through a familiar banking interface, but they are not necessarily taking direct self-custody of their Bitcoin or Ethereum.

For mainstream adoption, this type of arrangement could remove one of the biggest barriers facing traditional investors: the technical complexity of managing digital assets.

---

## There Is Another Important Advantage: Tax Management

One particularly interesting part of Discount Bank's offering involves tax treatment.

The bank says customers can offset capital gains and losses within their investment portfolios, helping consolidate tax calculations and withholding at source.

This could simplify the process for investors who previously had to manage cryptocurrency transactions separately from their traditional investments.

However, customers should understand the applicable tax rules and the bank's conditions before trading.

---

## Who Can Trade Bitcoin Through Discount Bank?

The service is not automatically available to everyone.

According to the published eligibility conditions:

- Customers must be at least 18 years old.
- The service is intended for private customers.
- Customers need an account enabled for securities trading.
- Participation is subject to the bank's terms and conditions.

The bank has also warned that cryptocurrency investments involve substantial risks, including significant price volatility and the possibility of losing the entire investment.

This is particularly important because buying cryptocurrency through a traditional bank does not eliminate the risks associated with Bitcoin and Ethereum.

---

## Why This Is a Big Moment for Israeli Crypto Adoption

The development also puts Discount Bank ahead of a larger competitor in the race to introduce direct cryptocurrency trading through Israeli banking apps.

Bank Leumi previously announced plans to offer Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana trading through its Leumi Trade platform in partnership with Galaxy Digital.

However, its service is expected to arrive in early 2027.

Discount Bank's November 26 launch therefore gives it an early position in Israel's emerging bank-based crypto trading market.

The significance goes beyond one bank.

For years, cryptocurrency adoption has largely depended on specialist exchanges, payment providers and digital asset companies. Now, traditional financial institutions are beginning to explore ways of bringing these assets directly into their existing customer services.

If more banks follow this approach, accessing Bitcoin could eventually become as familiar as purchasing stocks through a banking application.

---

## Could Other Banks Follow the Same Path?

That remains an important question.

Discount Bank's move could encourage other financial institutions to explore similar services, particularly as customer interest in digital assets continues to develop.

However, it is too early to conclude that every major bank will introduce cryptocurrency trading.

Banks still need to consider custody arrangements, regulatory requirements, customer protection, compliance and the financial risks associated with digital assets.

For now, Discount Bank has taken a notable step by making Bitcoin and Ethereum available through its banking platform.

The bigger story is not simply that another financial institution is supporting Bitcoin. It is that cryptocurrency is moving closer to the everyday banking experience.

---

## What This Means for Bitcoin

Discount Bank's launch is another sign that traditional banking and the cryptocurrency industry are moving closer together.

Bitcoin no longer needs to be accessed exclusively through specialist crypto platforms. At least in this Israeli banking example, customers can now access it through a familiar financial institution.

Whether this model spreads to other countries will be worth watching.
