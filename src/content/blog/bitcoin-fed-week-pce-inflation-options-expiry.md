The final week of October could put cryptocurrency markets to the test. From Tuesday, October 27, through Friday, October 30, investors will be watching the Federal Reserve, a fresh reading of US inflation, earnings from some of the world's largest technology companies and quarterly results from major crypto-related businesses.

The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) is scheduled to announce its latest interest-rate decision on Wednesday, October 28. A day later, the US government is due to publish September's Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) inflation report alongside its advance estimate of third-quarter gross domestic product (GDP).

Meanwhile, Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon are preparing to report quarterly results. Their combined market value exceeds $10 trillion, making their earnings important not only for equity investors but also for broader sentiment around artificial intelligence, technology spending and financial markets.

The week will conclude with a major Bitcoin derivatives event. Around $11 billion in Bitcoin options are scheduled to expire on Deribit on Friday, October 30.

For Bitcoin, the central question is whether monetary policy, inflation and corporate earnings will encourage investors to take on more risk—or push them toward a more cautious position.

## Why the Federal Reserve Decision Matters for Bitcoin

The Federal Reserve's October policy decision is likely to be the week's most closely watched macroeconomic event.

The FOMC meeting is scheduled for October 27–28, with the policy statement expected on Wednesday at 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time and the press conference with Fed Chair Jerome Powell at 2:30 p.m. ET. The dates are listed on the [Federal Reserve's official meeting calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm).

According to the market expectations cited in the current discussion, traders are assigning roughly a 90% probability to the Fed leaving interest rates unchanged. That probability is a market estimate, not a guarantee of the committee's decision, and it can change as economic information arrives.

For cryptocurrency investors, the decision itself may be only part of the story. The Fed's explanation of the economic outlook could have an equally important effect on markets.

Interest rates influence the cost of borrowing, the return available from lower-risk assets and the amount of risk investors are willing to take. When markets expect lower rates, some investors become more willing to allocate capital to assets such as Bitcoin. When rates are expected to remain elevated for longer, risk appetite can weaken.

However, Bitcoin does not respond to interest rates in a consistent or automatic way. Other factors—including ETF flows, liquidity, leverage, the US dollar and crypto-specific developments—can strengthen or offset the effect of monetary policy.

### Three possible outcomes from the Fed meeting

**1. The Fed holds rates and sounds cautious**

A decision to maintain rates could already be reflected in market prices. If policymakers emphasise persistent inflation or indicate that rate cuts remain distant, investors could interpret the statement as restrictive.

That could put pressure on risk-sensitive assets, particularly if Treasury yields or the US dollar rise following the announcement.

**2. The Fed holds rates but signals a more supportive outlook**

If policymakers indicate that inflation is easing and that the economic outlook allows for future monetary easing, markets could react positively.

Such a response could support equities and cryptocurrency prices, although the reaction would depend on how the statement compares with expectations before the meeting.

**3. The Fed surprises markets**

A decision that differs from expectations—or unexpected guidance about future policy—could trigger a more abrupt repricing across financial markets.

For Bitcoin traders, the speed of that repricing may be particularly important because leveraged positions can amplify price moves.

The most useful signal may therefore come from the combination of the policy statement, Powell's comments and how bond yields respond afterward.

## September PCE Inflation Could Complicate the Outlook

The next major event arrives on Thursday, October 29, when the US Bureau of Economic Analysis is scheduled to release September's Personal Income and Outlays report at 8:30 a.m. ET. The release includes the PCE price index, the Federal Reserve's preferred measure of consumer inflation. The schedule is confirmed by the [Bureau of Economic Analysis](https://www.bea.gov/news/schedule).

Market commentary has highlighted a potential headline PCE inflation reading of 3.6% year over year, which would remain substantially above the Fed's 2% longer-run inflation objective. That figure is an expectation, not an announced result.

The previous available report showed that headline PCE inflation rose 3.4% year over year in August 2026. Core PCE inflation, which excludes food and energy, increased 3.0% over the same period, according to the BEA's [August PCE report](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-august-2026).

The September figures will help investors assess whether inflation is moving closer to the Fed's target or remaining stubbornly elevated.

### What a hotter inflation reading could mean

If headline or core inflation comes in above expectations, investors may conclude that the Fed has less room to ease monetary policy.

That could increase expectations for interest rates to remain higher for longer. Treasury yields and the dollar could strengthen, potentially creating headwinds for Bitcoin and other risk assets.

A higher-than-expected inflation figure would not automatically mean the Fed will raise rates at its next meeting. Policymakers consider a range of economic indicators, and one report is unlikely to determine the entire policy outlook.

### What a cooler reading could mean

If inflation comes in below expectations, markets may become more confident that price pressures are easing.

That could support expectations for future monetary easing and improve sentiment toward risk-sensitive assets, including cryptocurrencies.

But a softer reading would not guarantee a Bitcoin rally. Investors would still need to consider economic growth, labour-market conditions, positioning and whether the result was already reflected in prices.

**The key distinction is between the inflation number itself and the difference between that number and market expectations.** Markets frequently react more strongly to surprises than to data that confirms an existing forecast.

## Third-Quarter GDP Adds Another Layer to the Market Picture

Thursday's US economic calendar also includes the advance estimate of third-quarter GDP, scheduled for 8:30 a.m. ET on October 29.

GDP provides a broad measure of economic activity. Investors will examine the report for evidence about consumer spending, business investment and the overall strength of the US economy.

A stronger-than-expected result could support confidence in economic growth. However, if strong growth coincides with persistent inflation, investors may also worry that the Fed will have fewer reasons to lower rates.

A weaker result could strengthen expectations for monetary easing, but it could also raise concerns about economic momentum.

That creates a complicated relationship for Bitcoin. Lower interest-rate expectations can be supportive, yet deteriorating growth and rising financial stress can also cause investors to reduce exposure to volatile assets.

The market's reaction will depend on how GDP, inflation and the Fed's guidance fit together.

### Why Microsoft matters

Microsoft's results will give investors another opportunity to assess demand for its cloud services and the commercial impact of artificial intelligence.

Investors are likely to examine cloud growth, AI-related revenue, capital expenditure and management's outlook for future investment.

Strong results and constructive guidance could support confidence in large technology companies. However, investors may react negatively if spending rises faster than revenue or if management signals slower growth.

### What Alphabet's earnings could reveal

Alphabet's results will offer insight into its advertising business, Google Cloud and the financial performance of its AI investments.

The market will be watching whether AI products and infrastructure are contributing meaningfully to revenue and whether investment levels remain sustainable.

Because Alphabet operates across search, advertising, cloud computing and AI, its results may influence expectations about the broader technology sector.

### Why Amazon is important

Amazon's earnings will provide information about cloud-computing demand, online retail performance and investment in AI infrastructure.

Its AWS business is particularly relevant to the market's assessment of enterprise technology spending.

If cloud demand remains strong, investors may interpret that as evidence that businesses continue to invest in digital infrastructure. If growth disappoints or spending pressures profitability, sentiment could weaken.

### The connection between Big Tech and Bitcoin

Technology stocks and Bitcoin are different assets with different fundamentals. Nevertheless, both can be affected by shifts in liquidity, interest-rate expectations and investor appetite for risk.

Strong technology earnings could support broader market confidence. Disappointing results, especially alongside restrictive Fed guidance, could encourage investors to reduce exposure to volatile assets.

The reaction will not necessarily be uniform. Bitcoin can move independently of equities when crypto-specific factors dominate.

For that reason, traders should treat Big Tech earnings as an important part of the market backdrop rather than a direct signal for Bitcoin's next move.

## Coinbase and Strategy Earnings Bring the Crypto Sector Into Focus

The earnings calendar becomes even more relevant for cryptocurrency investors on Thursday, October 29, when Coinbase and Strategy are also expected to report, according to the schedule highlighted in market commentary.

Their results could provide insight into two different parts of the crypto ecosystem.

Coinbase is a cryptocurrency trading and infrastructure business. Investors will be looking for information about trading activity, transaction revenue, subscription and services revenue, and demand for its broader platform.

A period of higher crypto prices does not necessarily guarantee stronger earnings. Trading volumes, customer activity, fee levels and the mix of services all influence financial performance.

Strategy, meanwhile, is closely associated with its Bitcoin holdings and its approach to financing Bitcoin accumulation.

Investors may focus on its Bitcoin position, financing activity, capital structure and disclosures about the relationship between its share price and the value of its underlying holdings.

The two companies offer different perspectives: Coinbase provides information about activity across the crypto business, while Strategy offers insight into a corporate model closely tied to Bitcoin exposure.

Their results could influence sentiment around the sector, but neither company's earnings alone will determine Bitcoin's market direction.

## Around $11 Billion in Bitcoin Options Expire on Friday

The final major event is scheduled for Friday, October 30, when approximately $11 billion in Bitcoin options are expected to expire on Deribit.

Market reports have identified the expiry as one of the largest concentrations of Bitcoin options open interest on the exchange this month. The reported notional value can change as traders open, close or adjust positions.

It is important to understand what that number represents. The $11 billion figure refers to the underlying value associated with the contracts, not $11 billion in cash that will automatically enter or leave the Bitcoin market.

Options give traders the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an asset at a specified price under the contract's terms. Call options are generally used to express upside exposure, while put options can provide downside exposure or hedging.

As expiry approaches, traders and market makers may adjust their positions. Depending on the size and distribution of those positions, hedging activity can contribute to short-term price volatility.

However, a large options expiry does not automatically cause a crash or a rally.

### Why the expiry matters

A substantial concentration of open interest can make certain price levels important to market participants.

If Bitcoin moves sharply before expiry, traders may adjust hedges or close positions. Those adjustments can interact with spot and futures markets.

The effect depends on several variables, including the distribution of calls and puts, strike prices, dealer positioning, liquidity and how much open interest remains close to expiry.

The options market also provides information about how traders are positioning for potential price moves. But open interest alone does not reveal whether every position is bullish or bearish, because each options contract has both a buyer and a seller.

The correct interpretation is that the expiry could be a source of volatility—not that Bitcoin is destined to move toward a particular price.

## What Could This Mean for Bitcoin?

The events scheduled this week create several possible scenarios for Bitcoin, but no single outcome is guaranteed.

### Bullish scenario: Inflation cools and the Fed sounds supportive

A softer-than-expected PCE reading, a Fed statement that leaves room for future easing and solid technology earnings could improve market sentiment.

If that happens alongside healthy spot demand and supportive derivatives positioning, Bitcoin could benefit from increased risk appetite.

Still, a positive macroeconomic backdrop would not guarantee a sustained rally. Traders would need to see how the market responds after the initial headlines.

### Bearish scenario: Inflation stays elevated and financial conditions tighten

If inflation exceeds expectations and the Fed signals that rates may remain restrictive, investors could reassess their expectations for monetary easing.

Higher yields, a stronger dollar or a decline in technology stocks could create headwinds for Bitcoin.

The impact could be amplified if leveraged traders are forced to reduce positions during a rapid price move.

### Mixed scenario: Events send conflicting signals

Markets may also receive a combination of positive and negative information.

For example, technology earnings could beat expectations while inflation remains stubbornly high. Alternatively, inflation could cool while corporate guidance disappoints.

In such circumstances, Bitcoin's direction may depend more on positioning, liquidity and crypto-specific flows than on any individual announcement.

This is why traders should avoid treating the calendar as a simple prediction of whether BTC will rise or fall.

## What Bitcoin Traders Should Watch Before Friday

With several major events packed into four days, the sequence of announcements matters.

First, traders will assess whether the Fed's decision matches expectations and whether Powell's comments change the outlook for December.

Next, the PCE inflation and GDP reports will help investors evaluate the balance between price pressures and economic growth.

Corporate earnings will provide another test of investor confidence, particularly around AI spending, cloud demand and cryptocurrency-related business activity.

Finally, traders will monitor Bitcoin's spot price, trading volume, funding rates and options positioning as Friday's expiry approaches.

The most useful information will come from the interaction between these factors. A headline may initially push Bitcoin in one direction, only for the move to reverse as traders reassess the broader implications.

For investors, the priority is to separate confirmed economic releases from forecasts, and market positioning from actual price direction.

## Conclusion: A High-Stakes Week, but No Guaranteed Bitcoin Breakout

The week of October 27–30 brings together several events that could shape financial-market sentiment: a Federal Reserve decision, US inflation and GDP data, earnings from Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon, results from Coinbase and Strategy, and a major Bitcoin options expiry.

Each event offers a different piece of information about the market environment.

The Fed will provide guidance on monetary policy. PCE inflation will help investors assess progress toward price stability. GDP will offer a fresh view of economic growth. Corporate earnings will reveal how businesses are performing, while the options expiry will bring attention to derivatives positioning.

Together, these developments could create conditions for heightened volatility in Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies.

But a busy calendar does not guarantee a breakout. The market's reaction will depend on whether the results surprise investors, how expectations change and whether actual buying or selling follows.

For Bitcoin traders, this is a week to watch the data, understand the risks and avoid assuming that any single event will determine the market's direction.
