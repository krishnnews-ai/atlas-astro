Bitcoin's October performance has left cryptocurrency investors with more questions than answers. The world's largest cryptocurrency ended the month below $80,000, while Ethereum suffered a considerably deeper decline. Although both assets attempted to recover at the beginning of November, uncertainty surrounding the US midterm elections, institutional investment flows and a major Binance security incident continues to influence market sentiment.

Bitcoin closed October down approximately 7.32%, while Ethereum recorded a monthly decline of 16.06%, according to CoinGlass figures reported by Incrypted. The losses were a significant setback for a market that had entered October with expectations of another strong month.

The recovery attempt on November 2 has offered some relief. Bitcoin moved back towards $79,000 after spending time below $78,000, while Ethereum also recorded gains. However, the broader market has not yet demonstrated that the October correction is over.

Investors are now watching several developments that could influence the next major move, including the US midterm elections on November 3, economic data, Federal Reserve policy expectations and the continuing investigation into the Binance incident.

The central question is whether Bitcoin can reclaim $80,000 and establish a stronger recovery, or whether the market will face another period of selling pressure.

## Bitcoin Ends October Below $80,000 After a Difficult Month

October did not deliver the performance many cryptocurrency traders had expected.

Bitcoin ended the month approximately 7.32% lower, according to CoinGlass data. This made October 2026 one of the cryptocurrency's weakest October performances in recent years.

The result was particularly disappointing because October is traditionally associated with positive Bitcoin returns. The crypto community often refers to the month as "Uptober", reflecting its historical tendency to deliver gains.

That seasonal pattern failed to provide much support this time.

Bitcoin's decline also came amid pressure across broader financial markets. Investors were assessing technology stock weakness, changing expectations around interest rates and uncertainty about the outlook for risk-sensitive assets.

The final days of October added another source of concern following the reported Binance security breach, which increased attention on cryptocurrency exchange security and market confidence.

However, no single development fully explains Bitcoin's monthly decline. The market was already dealing with several overlapping pressures.

## Why the $80,000 Bitcoin Level Matters

The $80,000 level has become an important psychological reference point for Bitcoin traders.

Round-number price levels often attract attention because investors use them to assess market direction, place orders and manage risk. When Bitcoin trades above $80,000, buyers may interpret it as evidence of improving momentum. A sustained move below the level can have the opposite effect.

The monthly close below $80,000 has therefore become an important technical development.

An earlier technical assessment discussed by Incrypted highlighted the significance of the monthly close around this level. The analysis considered a move towards approximately $79,500 as part of a bearish trading scenario and identified lower price areas that traders could monitor if selling pressure continued.

However, a monthly close below a psychological level does not automatically mean that a larger decline is inevitable.

For a stronger recovery signal, traders will want to see Bitcoin reclaim $80,000 and maintain that level rather than briefly moving above it.

Trading volume, spot-market demand and the formation of higher lows will also matter.

## Bitcoin Recovers Towards $79,000 as November Begins

Despite the weak October close, Bitcoin showed signs of stabilisation at the beginning of November.

On November 2, Bitcoin traded around the $78,000–$79,000 area after recovering from the previous week's market sell-off. The rebound followed three consecutive days of gains, according to market reporting.

The move offered some relief to investors who had watched Bitcoin fall towards the mid-$70,000 range during the October correction.

But the recovery remains relatively modest compared with the losses recorded during the month.

The $79,000 area is now an important short-term reference, while $80,000 remains a larger psychological threshold.

A sustained move above both levels could improve sentiment. Failure to maintain the recovery could leave Bitcoin vulnerable to another test of lower support.

For now, the market appears to be attempting to stabilise rather than confirming a fresh bullish trend.

## Ethereum Falls 16.06% as Altcoins Face Greater Pressure

Ethereum experienced a considerably weaker October than Bitcoin.

CoinGlass figures reported by Incrypted showed that ETH declined approximately 16.06% during the month, more than twice Bitcoin's percentage loss.

The difference highlights the pressure facing the broader altcoin market.

Ethereum is closely watched because of its role in decentralised finance, stablecoins, tokenization and smart-contract applications. However, strong long-term network activity does not necessarily translate into positive short-term price performance.

When investors become cautious, capital can move towards assets they consider relatively more established or liquid. This can leave Ethereum and smaller cryptocurrencies under greater pressure.

Ethereum's recovery attempt at the beginning of November will therefore be important.

Traders are watching whether ETH can establish support, recover recently lost levels and improve its performance relative to Bitcoin.

A sustained improvement in ETH/BTC could indicate that investors are becoming more willing to take risk beyond Bitcoin.

Until that happens, Ethereum's larger October decline remains an important warning sign for the altcoin market.

## US Midterm Elections Put Crypto Investors on Alert

The US midterm elections scheduled for November 3 are another major event attracting attention from cryptocurrency investors.

The elections will determine the balance of power in Congress and could influence the future direction of US financial and digital asset legislation.

Cryptocurrency policy has become an increasingly important issue in Washington. Lawmakers have debated market-structure rules, stablecoin regulation and the responsibilities of financial regulators overseeing digital assets.

A political outcome that supports clearer cryptocurrency rules could improve confidence among some businesses and institutional investors.

However, elections do not automatically produce immediate regulatory changes.

Even when lawmakers support digital asset legislation, proposals must still pass through the legislative process. Political disagreements and competing priorities can delay implementation.

For Bitcoin and Ethereum, the immediate impact may therefore come through investor expectations and market sentiment rather than a sudden change in regulation.

Traders will be watching election results and statements from policymakers for indications of how the next Congress could approach the cryptocurrency industry.

## Bitcoin Faces Three Possible Scenarios in November

Bitcoin's position below $80,000 has created several possible paths for the market. The next move will depend on whether buyers can establish stronger support or sellers regain control.

### 1. Bitcoin Reclaims $80,000 and Extends Its Recovery

The bullish scenario would involve Bitcoin moving above $80,000 and maintaining that level.

A stronger recovery would be more convincing if it came with increasing spot-market demand, higher trading volume and a series of higher lows.

Such a move could encourage investors to reconsider bearish positions established during October.

It could also improve sentiment across Ethereum and the wider altcoin market.

However, a brief move above $80,000 would not be enough to confirm a lasting trend reversal. Buyers would need to defend the level during subsequent market tests.

### 2. Bitcoin Remains Between $77,000 and $80,000

A second possibility is continued consolidation.

Bitcoin could remain within a relatively narrow range while traders wait for clearer signals from the US elections, economic data and institutional investment flows.

Consolidation would not necessarily be negative. After a difficult month, a period of sideways trading could allow the market to absorb selling pressure.

However, the longer Bitcoin remains unable to reclaim important resistance levels, the more cautious investors may become.

### 3. Bitcoin Falls Towards Lower Support Areas

The bearish scenario would involve Bitcoin failing to maintain its recent recovery and returning to lower price levels.

An earlier technical analysis discussed by Incrypted identified approximately $72,500 and the $70,000 area as potential downside reference points under a continued bearish structure.

These levels are scenarios to monitor, not guaranteed price targets.

A renewed decline could be triggered by weaker institutional demand, additional negative developments in the cryptocurrency sector or a deterioration in broader financial market sentiment.

The market will need to show whether buyers are willing to defend the current price area before traders can confidently assess the next direction.

## ETF Outflows Add Another Challenge for Bitcoin

Institutional investment flows are also playing an important role in the market outlook.

US spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds recorded approximately $643 million in net outflows on October 30, according to figures reported by market data providers.

The withdrawals marked a significant reversal from the earlier period of inflows and added pressure during an already difficult trading session.

Spot Ethereum ETFs also recorded substantial withdrawals during October.

ETF flows matter because they provide one indication of institutional and traditional financial market demand for cryptocurrency exposure.

However, daily outflows should not be interpreted as proof that all institutional investors are abandoning Bitcoin.

Investors may adjust positions for several reasons, including portfolio rebalancing, risk management and expectations around market volatility.

The important question for November is whether ETF demand stabilises and returns to positive territory.

A sustained improvement in flows could support market confidence, while continued withdrawals may make a recovery more difficult.

## Binance Security Incident Adds to Market Uncertainty

The cryptocurrency market also entered November dealing with the consequences of the major Binance security incident reported on October 29.

The incident involved an estimated $6.3 billion in stolen cryptocurrency, according to reporting on the breach.

Binance said it was working with law enforcement and industry partners to investigate the incident and address its financial impact.

The security event attracted considerable attention because of its scale and potential implications for exchange security.

Large cryptocurrency incidents can affect investor confidence, particularly when they involve major trading platforms. They can also increase scrutiny of wallet security, asset custody and transaction monitoring.

On November 2, Reuters reported that Bitcoin and Ether had recovered modestly following the previous week's incident. Binance also said customer withdrawals were stabilising as services resumed.

The breach remains an important part of the market backdrop, although it should not be treated as the only reason for October's price decline.

## What Could Decide Ethereum's Next Move?

Ethereum's recovery will depend on more than a short-term increase in its dollar price.

There are several indicators traders may monitor.

**Support and resistance:** ETH needs to demonstrate that buyers can defend important price areas and prevent another breakdown.

**Performance against Bitcoin:** A stronger ETH/BTC ratio could suggest that capital is gradually returning to Ethereum relative to Bitcoin.

**Spot-market demand:** Recovery supported by actual buying activity would generally be more convincing than a short-lived move driven by leveraged positions.

**Broader market sentiment:** Ethereum could benefit if Bitcoin stabilises and investors become more comfortable taking risk across the altcoin market.

Ethereum's role in DeFi, stablecoins and tokenized financial applications remains part of its long-term investment case. But those developments do not guarantee an immediate price recovery.

Investors should distinguish between the long-term development of the Ethereum ecosystem and the short-term behaviour of its market price.
