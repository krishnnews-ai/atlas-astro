Imagine making your first video at the age of nine, building a robot at 12, launching an AI startup as a teenager and eventually taking a selfie with Russian President Vladimir Putin.

Sounds like a movie? But this is the real-life journey of Raul John Aju, a young technology entrepreneur from Kerala, India.

Raul has suddenly become an international talking point after meeting Putin at the AI Journey 2026 conference in Moscow on November 26. However, his biggest achievement may not be that photograph. It is what he has managed to build before even finishing school.

---

## From Bathroom Videos to Building Robots

Raul's technology journey started in a rather unexpected place — his bathroom.

At just nine years old, he began experimenting with video editing and creating videos for fun. His interest grew after his father joined Adobe, giving him access to professional editing software.

What began as a childhood hobby soon developed into a serious interest in technology and artificial intelligence.

By the age of 12, Raul had already built a robot called **MeBot** for a school science project.

For many children, a school project ends when the marks are received. For Raul, it was just the beginning.

---

## 15 AI Tools and His Own Technology Company

Raul did not stop after building his first robot. He went on to develop around 15 AI-powered tools and establish his own technology venture, AIRealm Technologies.

Some of his notable projects include:

- **MeBot:** His early robotics creation developed as a school project.
- **RESQ AI:** An AI-based initiative focused on emergency-related assistance.
- **ZapGap:** One of the tools in his growing AI portfolio.
- **Investo AI:** A project exploring artificial intelligence in investment-related applications.
- **FeedFye:** Another product developed as part of his technology experiments.

He also created **ThinkCraft**, a platform designed to make AI learning more accessible and engaging.

His approach is simple but ambitious: AI should not remain something that only technology experts understand. Ordinary people, students and professionals should also be able to use it.

---

## Nearly 5 Lakh People Have Learned AI Through His Initiatives

Perhaps the most impressive part of Raul's journey is his work in AI education.

Through ThinkCraft Academy, he has reportedly helped nearly five lakh people learn about artificial intelligence.

His initiatives have reached students and working professionals, including learners connected with universities, educational institutions and corporate organisations.

His startup reportedly has around 15 employees, with some working remotely from other locations.

And there is another detail that makes his business story unusual.

**His father is also his employee!**

Raul's father, Aju Joseph, reportedly receives a monthly salary of Rs 2 lakh for working with his son's company.

With professional experience at organisations including Amazon, Adobe, Wipro and IBM, his father brings valuable business and corporate experience to the young entrepreneur's venture.

Raul has explained that technical knowledge can be learned through technology, but his father's professional experience is something he wants to learn directly from him.

---

## From Kerala to a Selfie With Vladimir Putin

The biggest international moment arrived in Moscow.

On November 26, Raul attended the AI Journey 2026 conference, where he got the opportunity to meet Russian President Vladimir Putin and Kazakhstan President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev.

The meeting quickly attracted attention after Raul shared his photograph with Putin on social media.

Reflecting on his journey, Raul described how he had gone from making his first video at nine to standing alongside the Russian president.

His participation also attracted attention from the Russian Embassy in India, which highlighted him as a schoolboy behind the ThinkCraft platform.

Before Moscow, Raul had also participated in the India AI Impact Summit 2026 in New Delhi, where he interacted with United Nations Secretary-General António Guterres.

---

## The Next Big Name in India's AI Generation?

What makes Raul's story interesting is not simply his age or his photograph with a world leader.

It is the combination of curiosity, experimentation and entrepreneurship.

He started with video editing, moved into robotics, developed AI tools, built a company and began teaching others about artificial intelligence.

Of course, building a successful global technology company takes much more than early achievements. But Raul has already managed to do something unusual: turn a childhood interest into a serious business journey.

His meeting with Putin may be the photograph everyone is talking about today.

But the bigger story is what this young Indian innovator chooses to build tomorrow.

**And at this stage, his journey is only getting started.**

---

## The Bottom Line

Raul John Aju, a teenage AI entrepreneur from Kerala, built 15 AI tools, founded AIRealm Technologies, and helped nearly 5 lakh people learn AI through ThinkCraft Academy.

His father works as his employee, bringing corporate experience from Amazon, Adobe, Wipro, and IBM.

He met Vladimir Putin at the AI Journey 2026 conference in Moscow and previously interacted with UN Secretary-General António Guterres.

His story is a testament to how curiosity, experimentation, and early entrepreneurship can lead to extraordinary achievements — even before finishing school.
