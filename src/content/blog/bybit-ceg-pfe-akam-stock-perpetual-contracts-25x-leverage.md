Bybit has expanded its traditional finance derivatives offering with three new stock-linked perpetual contracts, giving eligible users access to price movements associated with Constellation Energy, Pfizer and Akamai Technologies through USDT-settled products.

The exchange announced the listings on November 3, introducing CEGUSDT, PFEUSDT and AKAMUSDT under its TradFi Perpetual product lineup. According to Bybit's individual listing notices, all three contracts support maximum leverage of up to 25x and are designed to trade around the clock.

The additions bring companies from three different industries into Bybit's derivatives environment: energy, pharmaceuticals and internet infrastructure. Rather than purchasing shares through a conventional brokerage account, traders can use the contracts to take positions based on movements in the referenced equity prices.

The distinction is important. These products are synthetic derivatives, not direct share purchases. Holding a position does not give traders ownership of Constellation Energy, Pfizer or Akamai stock, or the shareholder rights associated with those companies.

## Three New TradFi Perpetual Contracts Explained

The latest listings cover three publicly traded companies with different business profiles.

**1. CEGUSDT – Constellation Energy**

The CEGUSDT contract references Constellation Energy, a major U.S. energy company. Its business exposure makes the stock relevant to market discussions involving electricity demand, power generation and the broader energy sector.

**2. PFEUSDT – Pfizer**

The PFEUSDT contract tracks the price movements of Pfizer, one of the world's best-known pharmaceutical companies. Pfizer's market performance can be influenced by drug sales, clinical developments, regulatory decisions, earnings and the wider healthcare industry.

**3. AKAMUSDT – Akamai Technologies**

The AKAMUSDT contract references Akamai Technologies, a company associated with content delivery, cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity services. Its business operates in areas connected to internet performance and digital security.

The three listings give traders exposure to different areas of the U.S. equity market through a single derivatives platform. However, the contracts remain separate instruments, each with its own underlying reference asset and market risks.

## Bybit Listing Details: Leverage, Settlement and Trading Hours

Bybit's official announcements provide a common set of specifications for the three new contracts.

The availability of these products may vary by jurisdiction and account eligibility.

## Why Stock-Linked Perpetual Contracts Matter

Traditional stock markets operate within exchange-specific trading sessions. Derivatives platforms, however, are increasingly offering products that allow users to trade exposure to traditional financial assets through crypto-style infrastructure.

Stock-linked perpetual contracts are part of this convergence between traditional finance and digital-asset trading.

For users, the model can provide several features:

- Exposure to selected equity price movements without directly buying the shares.
- USDT-based settlement instead of conventional stock-market settlement.
- The ability to take long or short positions, subject to the platform's product rules.
- Access to trading outside standard stock-market hours.
- Integration with supported derivatives trading tools and automated strategies.

These features may appeal to traders who already use cryptocurrency derivatives platforms and want to follow traditional financial markets through a familiar trading interface.

However, around-the-clock trading does not mean the underlying stock market is open 24/7. The contract's pricing and liquidity may be affected by the availability of reference-market data, market closures, volatility and the exchange's own risk controls.

## Why the Difference Between Stock Ownership and Derivatives Matters

One of the most important details in Bybit's announcement is the legal and financial structure of its TradFi Perpetual products.

The contracts are synthetic derivatives designed to reference the price movements of underlying assets. They do not transfer ownership of the actual shares to traders.

That means a CEGUSDT position does not make its holder a Constellation Energy shareholder. The same applies to positions in PFEUSDT and AKAMUSDT.

Traders do not receive shareholder voting rights, dividends or other benefits attached to owning the underlying securities through these contracts.

There can also be differences between the contract's market price and the price of the referenced stock. As a result, users should not assume that the derivative will always move in perfect alignment with the underlying equity.

This distinction is especially relevant for investors who are accustomed to purchasing stocks through regulated brokerage accounts. A perpetual contract offers a different type of exposure and carries a different risk profile.

## How 25x Leverage Can Affect Traders

Bybit has set the maximum leverage for the three new contracts at 25x.

Leverage allows traders to control a larger position relative to the margin they commit. However, it also increases the impact of price movements on their account balance.

For illustration, a trader using 25x leverage could control a position worth $2,500 with $100 in initial margin, before fees and other requirements.

A relatively small adverse movement in the underlying reference price can therefore produce a substantial loss compared with the margin committed. If the position falls below the platform's maintenance-margin requirements, it may be liquidated.

The maximum leverage is not a recommended trading level. Actual leverage availability can depend on position size, margin requirements, account restrictions and Bybit's risk controls.

The contracts also use funding payments at eight-hour intervals, with a published funding-rate cap of ±2%. Funding payments can affect the cost of maintaining an open position, depending on the applicable rate and position direction.

## Futures Bots Extend the Trading Options

Bybit also stated that the three contracts would be available through supported Futures Bots.

The named tools include:

- **Futures Grid:** A strategy that places orders across a defined price range.
- **Futures Martingale:** A strategy that adjusts position size according to predefined rules after market movements.
- **Futures Combo:** A tool for combining supported futures trading strategies.

Automation can help users execute predefined trading rules, but it does not remove market risk. Strategies can perform poorly during sharp price movements, low-liquidity conditions or unexpected market events.

Users should understand each bot's settings, exposure and liquidation risks before enabling automated trading.
