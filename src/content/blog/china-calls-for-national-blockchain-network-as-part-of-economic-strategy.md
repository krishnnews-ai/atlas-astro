**China has officially called for the construction of a national blockchain network as a core component of its digital economy strategy. This state-led infrastructure initiative aims to anchor high-value data — such as cross-border trade and global payments — within a domestically controlled blockchain system.**

This marks a fundamental shift in China's positioning of blockchain technology: **from guarding against crypto asset risks to actively building and controlling it as national strategic infrastructure.**

***

### What Is the National Blockchain Network?

According to disclosures made in March 2026 by Dong Jin, a deputy to the National People's Congress and president of the Beijing Academy of Blockchain and Edge Computing, at the "Representative Channel," a national-level blockchain network has already taken shape. The core goal is to "wrap" high-value data in cross-border trade and global payments into China's self-controlled blockchain system.

By October 2026, the Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council formally issued guidelines explicitly proposing to **"build a nationally integrated computing power network and a national blockchain network,"** incorporating it into the top-level design for developing new quality productive forces.

### What Has Already Been Built?

This is not a concept starting from scratch — it is a massive system already in operation:

**Application Scale**

- Deployed across **16 central government ministries** and **27 centrally administered state-owned enterprises**
- Tax sector: Hundreds of billions of invoices are processed annually on the self-controlled blockchain network, ensuring authenticity and traceability
- Cross-border trade: Over **300,000 enterprises** have registered, with on-chain cross-border trade volume reaching **trillions of RMB**
- Global payments: The technology has been integrated with the central bank's digital yuan, opening new channels for cross-border transactions

**Core Technology**

- The world's first **96-core blockchain acceleration chip**, delivering a **50-fold performance increase**, solving the computational bottleneck of ultra-large-scale blockchain networks
- The world's first software-hardware integrated blockchain operating system, with **3 million lines of code open-sourced** for public use

Dong Jin described this as building a "**Trusted Digital Great Wall**" that connects the nation and links the world.

### China's Unique Path: Blockchain ≠ Cryptocurrency

The key to understanding this is that **China strictly separates blockchain technology from cryptocurrency**.

Beijing banned cryptocurrency trading and mining years ago, but has simultaneously been actively promoting blockchain as a tool for data management, supply chain tracking, and government services. This national blockchain network is fundamentally different from public, permissionless chains like Bitcoin and Ethereum — it is a **state-controlled, centrally managed permissioned infrastructure**.

This aligns with the "Xinghuo Chain Network" launched in 2020. The latter is a national blockchain data network guided by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and initiated by the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology. It has completed super node construction in Macau, Malaysia, and is advancing nodes in Hong Kong, Singapore, and beyond. The new national blockchain network may further expand or integrate on this foundation.

### What Does This Mean for the Market?

**Direct impact is limited.** Since this network is completely isolated from open cryptocurrency trading and token markets, such infrastructure announcements have historically had minimal impact on crypto markets.

**Indirect impact is worth watching.** Analysts may focus on whether this signals a new round of state investment spending on blockchain infrastructure, which could benefit domestic technology suppliers. At the same time, it provides a reference point for other major economies weighing centralized versus decentralized digital infrastructure paths — China's model represents a distinctly different choice.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this mean China has lifted its cryptocurrency ban?**
No. China has banned cryptocurrency trading and mining for years and has consistently distinguished blockchain technology from crypto assets.

**Is this network the same as the digital yuan?**
No. The digital yuan is a central bank digital currency, while the national blockchain network is separate infrastructure technology, though officials often discuss the two together.

**Can ordinary investors participate?**
This is a domestic infrastructure project for government agencies and enterprise deployment. It does not involve public token offerings or investment opportunities.

***

**Bottom line: What China is building is a state-controlled blockchain "trusted data network" serving the real economy and government governance, completely isolated from the open cryptocurrency ecosystem. This is a national-level strategic layout in digital infrastructure, not a bullish signal for crypto markets.**
