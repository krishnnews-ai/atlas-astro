Flipkart is entering a new leadership chapter, and one name is suddenly attracting attention: **Balaji Thiagarajan**.

After Kalyan Krishnamurthy announced that he would conclude his tenure as CEO, Flipkart confirmed that Thiagarajan and the company's board would lead the business on an interim basis. The company says a permanent successor will be announced in the coming weeks.

So, who is Balaji Thiagarajan? And could the man now helping lead Flipkart become its next permanent CEO?

---

## Who Is Balaji Thiagarajan?

Balaji Thiagarajan is an experienced technology executive who currently serves as Flipkart's Chief Product and Technology Officer (CPTO).

He is not a newcomer to the technology industry. His career includes senior roles at some of the world's biggest technology companies, including **Microsoft, Google, Yahoo and Uber**.

Before joining Flipkart, he also served as Chief Technology Officer at LendingClub and founded Kasu.ai, an AI-focused enterprise venture.

His professional background combines technology leadership, product development and experience working across large digital platforms — skills that could prove important as Flipkart prepares for its next phase of growth.

---

## From Microsoft and Google to Flipkart's Leadership

Thiagarajan's career stands out because of the companies he has worked with.

At Microsoft, he held a senior engineering role associated with Windows Live. He later worked at Google as a software engineering executive. His experience also includes Yahoo and Uber, where he worked on technology and platform-related responsibilities.

He studied Electrical and Electronics Engineering at the University of Pune, earned a master's degree in Computer Science from Syracuse University and completed an MBA at the Wharton School.

In September 2025, he joined Flipkart as its Chief Product and Technology Officer.

Now, following the leadership change, he has moved into a much bigger spotlight.

---

## Is Balaji Thiagarajan Flipkart's Next CEO?

That is the question many people will be watching closely.

For now, **Flipkart has not announced Thiagarajan as its permanent CEO**. The company has only confirmed that he and the board will lead the organisation during the transition.

His technology background and experience at major global companies make him an important figure in this leadership change. But whether he gets the top job permanently remains unknown.

Walmart and Flipkart have said that the successor will be announced in the coming weeks.

---

## What Happens Next for Flipkart?

The timing of this change makes the decision particularly interesting. Flipkart is one of India's biggest e-commerce platforms, with more than 500 million registered users, according to the company.

Its next leader will have to navigate intense competition, changing shopping habits, quick-commerce growth and the company's longer-term business ambitions.

For now, Balaji Thiagarajan is at the centre of the transition. Whether he becomes the permanent CEO or helps prepare the company for another leader, his role could be crucial in shaping Flipkart's next chapter.

**The big question remains: Is Flipkart's next CEO already inside the company, or does Walmart have another name in mind?**

## The Bottom Line

Balaji Thiagarajan, Flipkart's CPTO, is now leading the company on an interim basis after Kalyan Krishnamurthy's exit.

He brings a strong resume from Microsoft, Google, Yahoo, and Uber.

Flipkart has not confirmed him as the permanent CEO, and a successor will be announced in the coming weeks.

The big question: Is Flipkart's next CEO already in the building, or is Walmart looking outside?
