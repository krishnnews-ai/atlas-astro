## Key Takeaways

- Bitmine holds **6,016,414 ETH** — about **4.9%** of Ethereum's circulating supply.
- Tom Lee says purchases will stop at **5%** of circulating supply.
- Company needs roughly **100,000 more ETH** — about 6–7 weeks at last week's pace.
- Bitmine bought another **$41 million** worth of ETH last week.
- Company holds approximately **$643 million** in cash and marketable securities.
- ETH treasury strategy launched in **June 2025**, with weekly purchases since then.

***

**Bitmine Chairman Tom Lee** has drawn a clear line in the sand for the company's Ethereum accumulation strategy. In a recent statement, Lee said Bitmine will **stop purchasing ETH once its holdings reach 5% of the token's circulating supply**. The company is currently very close to that target.

According to the latest data, Bitmine holds **6,016,414 ETH**, which represents roughly **4.9% of Ethereum's circulating supply**. To hit the 5% mark, the company needs to acquire approximately **100,000 more ETH** — a target that could be reached in **six to seven weeks** at last week's buying pace.

## Why Tom Lee's 5% Target Matters

Tom Lee's decision to cap ETH purchases at 5% of circulating supply is significant for several reasons. First, it shows a **disciplined approach to treasury management** — rather than accumulating indefinitely, Bitmine has set a clear, measurable target.

Second, the 5% threshold is large enough to make Bitmine one of the **largest corporate holders of Ethereum** globally. For context, 5% of ETH's circulating supply is a substantial position that could influence market sentiment and liquidity.

> "We will stop accumulating ETH once our holdings reach 5% of the token's circulating supply. We are currently at roughly 4.9%."
> — Tom Lee, Chairman, Bitmine

***

## Bitmine's Ethereum Treasury Strategy

Bitmine launched its **Ethereum treasury strategy in June 2025** and has purchased ETH **every week since then**. This consistent buying pattern has made the company a notable player in the corporate crypto treasury space.

The strategy mirrors similar moves by other public companies that have added crypto to their balance sheets. However, Bitmine's approach is distinctive because of its **clear 5% cap** and weekly buying cadence.

***

## What's Next for Bitmine?

At last week's buying pace, Bitmine could reach its 5% target within **six to seven weeks**. Once that target is hit, the company is expected to **pause ETH purchases** and focus on managing its existing holdings.

With approximately **$643 million in cash and marketable securities**, Bitmine has ample liquidity to complete its remaining purchases and still maintain a strong balance sheet.
