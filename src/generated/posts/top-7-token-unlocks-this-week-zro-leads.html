1. LayerZero (ZRO): The Largest Unlock of the Week

LayerZero's ZRO ranks first in the weekly list with an estimated $26.8 million of tokens scheduled for release and a reported impact of 6.87% of circulating supply.

ZRO is therefore the largest unlock in the September 14–20 ranking by estimated dollar value. A release of this size can increase the amount of tradable supply available to the market, although an unlock does not automatically mean that all released tokens will immediately be sold.

Other tokenomics trackers have published somewhat different ZRO estimates. For clarity, the $26.8 million figure is the weekly-ranking estimate used in this article; other reports may show slightly different values depending on the token price and supply methodology used.

2. Bedrock (BR): Large Supply Impact Despite a Lower Dollar Value

Why Token Unlock Figures Differ Across Platforms

Investors often see different numbers for the same token unlock. That does not automatically mean that one source is incorrect. The most common reasons include token-price timing, circulating-supply snapshots, different denominators and whether a platform is reporting a single-day event, a weekly total or a broader monthly schedule.

Why Token Unlocks Matter for Crypto Traders

Token unlocks matter because they can change the amount of supply that becomes transferable or potentially available to the market. When a large allocation becomes unlocked, investors may reassess the balance between available supply and demand.

However, an unlock itself is not a guaranteed sell signal. Tokens may remain with investors, project contributors, foundations, ecosystem participants or other recipients. Some may be staked, transferred, held or used within the project's ecosystem.

The potential market effect depends on several factors:

  • Unlock size: Larger dollar-value releases can attract more market attention.
  • Percentage of supply: A smaller dollar unlock can still be significant if it represents a large share of the relevant supply.
  • Recipient type: Investor, team, foundation and ecosystem allocations can have different selling behavior.
  • Liquidity: A highly liquid token may absorb additional supply more easily than a thinly traded asset.
  • Market conditions: Risk appetite, Bitcoin's direction and broader crypto liquidity can amplify or reduce the effect.

This distinction is important for avoiding misleading headlines that combine events from different dates into a single weekly list.
