Trust Wallet CEO Eowyn Chen: Speed Isn't Just a Feature — It's a Shield Against Slippage

Bitcoin NewsMarket News

 


If you've ever made a trade on a decentralized exchange, you've probably felt the sting of slippage. You hit "confirm" on a swap, and the final price is noticeably worse than what you expected. A few seconds of delay can cost you real money.

Eowyn Chen, CEO of Trust Wallet, says that's exactly the problem her team is trying to solve. According to Chen, prioritizing speed on high-performance blockchains is critical for protecting users from execution price changes.

Chen's argument is straightforward: when you're trading on a fast chain, added latency can directly lead to a worse execution price. The longer it takes for your transaction to be picked up, the more time the market has to move against you.

Beyond Speed: The Security Balancing Act

Of course, speed isn't the only thing that matters. A wallet that's blazing fast but insecure is useless.

Trust Wallet had to navigate this delicate balance recently. Following a December 2025 security incident that impacted users of its browser extension, the company faced the challenge of fairly compensating affected users.

Chen emphasized that during the compensation process, accuracy took precedence over speed to ensure the system wasn't exploited by fraudulent claims. This highlights a key point: Trust Wallet is optimizing for speed in execution but prioritizing precision when it comes to security and user protection.

The Bigger Picture: The Wallet as the Gateway

Chen has previously spoken about the evolving role of crypto wallets. She envisions them as the primary interface for the digital economy, moving beyond simple storage. Users, she argues, won't care whether they're using crypto or fiat — they'll just want speed, transparency, and reliability.

By prioritizing speed on high-performance chains, Trust Wallet is making a bet on that future. The goal, as Chen puts it, is to make wallets feel like a trusted guide, where the user doesn't have to think about latency, network congestion, or slippage. The experience should just be fast and secure.

The Bottom Line

For the average crypto user, Chen's comments are a reminder that your wallet choice matters. A wallet that prioritizes speed can help reduce the impact of slippage on trades. And as the crypto space continues to evolve, the battle between speed and security will remain one of the defining challenges for wallet providers.
