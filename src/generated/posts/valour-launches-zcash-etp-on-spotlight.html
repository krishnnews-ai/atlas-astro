DeFi Technologies' subsidiary Valour has launched a Zcash (ZEC) exchange-traded product, giving traditional investors a regulated way to gain exposure to one of crypto's longest-running privacy-focused networks.

The VALOUR ZCASH (ZEC) SEK ETP began trading on the Spotlight Stock Market on September 29, 2026, under ISIN CH1108681763. The product carries a management fee of 1.9%.

What the ETP Does

The Zcash ETP provides price exposure to ZEC through traditional brokerage accounts. Investors don't need to purchase, store, or manage the underlying digital asset directly.

The product follows Valour's broader ETP model, providing investors with exchange-traded exposure to a digital asset without requiring them to hold the underlying cryptocurrency directly. The company now has more than 100 products spanning major digital assets, Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks, DeFi protocols, staking products, and emerging blockchain ecosystems.

"Zcash represents one of the most important early applications of zero-knowledge cryptography in digital assets, and interest in privacy-preserving blockchain infrastructure continues to grow," said Johan Wattenström, CEO of DeFi Technologies. "We are giving investors access to an established digital asset through the same regulated, familiar market infrastructure they use to access traditional securities."

What Is Zcash?

Zcash launched in 2016 as a decentralized digital currency designed to give users greater control over transaction privacy.

It became one of the earliest major real-world applications of zk-SNARK zero-knowledge proof technology, allowing certain transaction details to be verified without publicly revealing the underlying information. Zcash supports both shielded transactions, which protect transaction data, and transparent transactions, which work like typical public blockchain transactions.

ZEC is the network's native asset, used for transfers and transaction fees.

Why This Launch Matters

The launch adds another listed investment vehicle through which eligible investors can obtain exposure to ZEC without directly holding the cryptocurrency.

The Zcash ETP arrives at a time when privacy-preserving digital assets are getting more attention. The launch expands the range of digital assets available through Valour's ETP platform.

"Zcash gives users the ability to transact privately using zero-knowledge cryptography, and we have seen growing interest in ZEC from both professional and Nordic retail investors," said Jacob Lindberg, Chief Revenue Officer of Valour.

The Nordic market is a key focus for Valour. The company has been expanding across European and international markets, and the Zcash ETP is its latest addition to a platform that already covers the major digital assets.

What Traders Should Watch

The ETP simplifies access to ZEC through traditional market infrastructure, but it does not remove the risks associated with the underlying asset. Zcash remains a volatile digital asset and is also subject to evolving regulatory treatment, liquidity conditions, and market demand.

For investors interested in privacy-preserving blockchain technology, the ETP offers a regulated route. For those watching the broader ETP market, Valour's continued expansion signals that demand for regulated crypto products isn't slowing down.
