Key Takeaways:

  • Trader Bonk Guy called it a potential signal for an upcoming "BNB szn" rally.

  • CZ has previously denied having the ability to move crypto markets, calling past timing "coincidental."

  • Tom Lee responded to CZ's post with: "Agree, do you own enough crypto?"

  • CZ's post follows a bullish BNB Chain analysis highlighting tokenized U.S. equities, low gas fees, and 17 million daily transactions.

Binance founder Changpeng "CZ" Zhao is back in the spotlight. On Tuesday, CZ posted an AI-generated image of himself in bed with a green night-vision filter, looking drowsy and surprised, with a single caption: "Soon...."

The post amplified an earlier tweet praising BNB Chain's fundamentals. That analysis highlighted several key metrics: tokenized U.S. equities deployed on-chain with BNB Chain as a key participant, low gas fees, a block time of approximately 0.45 seconds, over 2 million daily active users, and around 17 million daily transactions .

The message was clear to some. Prominent trader Bonk Guy responded that CZ is likely hinting at a potential "BNB szn" rally, warning users: "The warning has been given—you just didn't notice" .

Tom Lee, Chairman of Bitmine Immersion Technologies, also weighed in. He endorsed CZ's post with a simple question: "Agree, do you own enough crypto?" .

What CZ Actually Said — and Didn't Say

CZ's "Soon...." post is deliberately vague. He didn't announce a specific catalyst, timeline, or price target. But that hasn't stopped the crypto community from reading into it.

The context matters. In August, CZ posted a message suggesting that if you want your future self to thank you for today's decision, you need to act immediately. Bitcoin was trading around $60,000 at the time. The crypto market rose roughly 20% over the following days .

When asked about that post, CZ denied having the ability to influence market prices. "I may post 5 to 10 times a day, so it is not surprising that one of them happened to appear before positive news," he said on the "When Shift Happens" podcast . He noted that Trump made crypto-positive remarks about five hours after his post, and he had no advance knowledge of the speech content.

He also emphasized: "If I possessed any information that could affect the market, I would instead not post about related content" .

Why BNB Chain Is Getting Attention

The bullish BNB Chain analysis that CZ retweeted wasn't just about price. It focused on fundamentals:

  • Tokenized U.S. equities are now on-chain, with BNB Chain among the key participants

  • Low gas fees and fast block times (0.45 seconds) make it competitive for high-frequency activity

  • Over 2 million daily active users and 17 million daily transactions

  • BNB token burns continue, with a supply target of 100 million

The analysis concluded that BNB Chain "will become a market focus by 2026" .

CZ's Track Record

CZ has made bold predictions before. In January 2026, he told CNBC at Davos that 2026 could be a "supercycle" for Bitcoin, citing growing U.S. government support for crypto and institutional capital entering through ETFs . He said he holds only Bitcoin and BNB personally and doesn't trade short-term .

He later acknowledged the supercycle could be "delayed" and at one point called the market a "crypto winter" . But his long-term conviction hasn't changed. In a Bali Clubhouse AMA in August, he said he still believes in the four-year Bitcoin cycle and sees similarities to the bear market four years ago .

What's Next

CZ's "Soon...." post doesn't guarantee anything. It could be a signal, a joke, or simply a meme. The crypto community is treating it as a possible harbinger of a BNB Chain rally — but the same community knows that CZ has both predicted and denied market influence in equal measure.

What's clear is that BNB Chain's fundamentals are improving. Whether that translates into a "BNB szn" depends on factors CZ can't control: market sentiment, liquidity, and macro conditions.

For now, traders are watching. And as Bonk Guy put it, the warning has been given — whether you noticed or not.
