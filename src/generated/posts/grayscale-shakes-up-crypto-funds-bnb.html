Grayscale Shakes Up Crypto Funds: BNB Enters, UNI and NEAR Exit in Q2 2026 Rebalance

Top Crypto News 2026-08-06T11:37:10.987Z
Market News

 



Grayscale's latest quarterly rebalance highlights a major shift toward BNB and AI-focused assets, signaling where institutional money is moving next.

📊 The Big Picture

Grayscale Investments, the world's largest digital asset manager, has completed its Q2 2026 multi-asset fund rebalancing, effective after market close on August 3 . The rebalance touched three key funds: the DeFi Fund, the Smart Contract Fund, and the Decentralized AI Fund .

The most significant move: BNB was added to the Smart Contract Fund for the first time, immediately becoming the fund's largest holding at 30.6% . Meanwhile, UNI was trimmed from the DeFi Fund, and NEAR was sold off from the AI Fund .

🔄 Smart Contract Fund: BNB Takes the Crown

The Smart Contract Fund (GSC) saw the most dramatic transformation. BNB now leads the portfolio at 30.6%, edging out Ethereum (29.47%) and Solana (29.15%) .

Analysts view this as a strong vote of confidence in the BNB Chain ecosystem . The move suggests institutional investors are now looking beyond traditional smart contract platforms like Ethereum and Solana toward the BNB ecosystem .

"The addition of BNB reveals that institutional investors are beginning to show increasing interest not only in traditional smart contract networks like Ethereum and Solana, but also in the BNB Chain ecosystem." — Market Analysts 

🏦 DeFi Fund: UNI Gets Trimmed, ONDO Rises

The DeFi Fund (DEFG) sold its UNI holdings and reallocated proceeds across other components . Despite the sale, UNI remains the largest holding at 34.16% .

The move comes amid growing concerns over DeFi exploits, with institutional strategies increasingly factoring in protocol risk . Ondo (ONDO), a protocol focused on tokenized real-world assets, climbed from 19.83% to 25.44%, reflecting growing institutional interest in RWA tokenization .

🤖 AI Fund: NEAR Out, TAO and RENDER In

The Decentralized AI Fund sold NEAR Protocol and reallocated proceeds . NEAR remains the largest holding at 31.35%, but its position was reduced .

Additionally, Sui (SUI) received a 1.9% weight in the AI Fund, replacing some of the NEAR allocation . The shift reflects a changing classification of layer-1 smart contract platforms with on-chain AI activity .

📉 Cardano Takes a Hit

Cardano (ADA) absorbed the sharpest cut across all funds. Its weight in the Smart Contract Fund fell from 17.96% in May to just 4.88% in August . Grayscale also slashed positions in Hedera (HBAR), Avalanche (AVAX), and Sui (SUI), each now below 2.1% .
