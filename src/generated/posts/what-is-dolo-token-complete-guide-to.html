If you've come across DOLO in your crypto journey and wondered what it actually is, you're not alone. DOLO is the native token of Dolomite, a decentralized finance protocol that launched on Arbitrum in 2022.

Here's what you need to know.

What Is Dolomite?

Dolomite is a DeFi money market protocol that launched on Arbitrum in 2022. It's a lending, borrowing and trading platform that operates across multiple blockchains, including Arbitrum, Mantle, Ethereum, and Berachain. Think of it as a money market where you can lend, borrow, and trade assets, but with a twist: it's designed to make your capital work harder.

The core idea: Instead of your assets sitting idle, Dolomite lets you use them as collateral while still earning yield. This is what the project calls "capital efficiency."

Key Features

  • Supported Assets: Over 1,000 different cryptoassets — significantly more than protocols like Aave or Compound
  • Multi-Chain: Available on Arbitrum, Mantle, Ethereum, and Berachain
  • Capital Efficiency: Use assets as collateral while still generating yield

The Three-Token System

One of the interesting things about Dolomite is that it doesn't just have one token. It has a three-token ecosystem, each with a specific job:

This separation means the governance token and the utility token aren't the same thing, which is different from many other DeFi projects.

How Dolomite Works

At its core, Dolomite is a lending, borrowing and trading protocol. Here's the basic flow:

  1. You deposit cryptoassets as collateral
  2. You can borrow stablecoins against that collateral
  3. While your assets are deposited, they can still earn yield through the protocol's integration with other DeFi platforms

The protocol uses Chainlink oracles to ensure fair pricing and includes a 5% liquidation penalty to manage risk.

What Makes It Different?

The main differentiator is asset diversity. While Aave and Compound primarily support major assets like ETH, USDC, and DAI, Dolomite supports over 1,000 different assets. This gives users more flexibility but also comes with higher risk, as less-liquid assets can be more volatile.

The World Liberty Financial Connection

Dolomite gained additional attention when its co-founder, Corey Caplan, was named an advisor to World Liberty Financial (WLFI) in September 2024. This created a strategic bridge between the two projects. The connection also brought WLFI-related assets into Dolomite's ecosystem, expanding the relationship between the two projects.

The connection increased attention around Dolomite, particularly as World Liberty Financial later used the protocol for lending and collateral activity.

Security and Risks

No DeFi protocol is without risk, and Dolomite has had its challenges.

In March 2024, a vulnerability in Dolomite's old Ethereum contracts, which had been deployed years earlier and discontinued, was exploited. Approximately $1.8 million was taken from 187 victims. Dolomite's own post-mortem says the vulnerable old system dated to 2019 and had been shut down in 2020; the incident affected users who still had old approvals.

Other risks to consider:

  • The protocol is still relatively small compared to established players
  • Support for over 1,000 assets means some may have low liquidity
  • The governance mechanism requires active participation to be effective

Should You Buy DOLO?

This isn't financial advice — it's context.

Dolomite offers a genuinely different approach to DeFi by focusing on asset diversity and capital efficiency. The integration with World Liberty Financial and a reported $3.4 million in total funding, with Coinbase Ventures among its investors, suggest institutional interest.

However, the project's smaller size, security history, and competitive landscape (Aave, Compound, and others) mean it's not without risk.

If you're considering DOLO:

  1. Understand that it's a governance and utility token, not a stablecoin
  2. Be aware of the difference between DOLO on Arbitrum/Ethereum and DOL on Avalanche
  3. Check the current TVL and trading volume to assess activity
  4. Remember that DeFi protocols can have smart contract risks

The Bottom Line

Dolomite (DOLO) is a DeFi lending protocol that stands out for its support of over 1,000 assets and its focus on capital efficiency. It has a three-token model, a connection to World Liberty Financial, and a history that includes both growth and a security incident.

As with any crypto project, do your own research. Understand the tokenomics, the team, and the risks before making any decisions.
