Fasset has reached a $1 billion valuation after raising $68 million in a Series C funding round led by Japan's SBI Group, pushing the stablecoin-focused neobanking platform into unicorn territory.

The financing was announced on August 24, 2026, and follows Fasset's $51 million Series B round in May. The latest raise takes the company's total fundraising for 2026 to $119 million.

Fasset says the new capital will be used to expand its regulated financial infrastructure, known as Own Network, while increasing investment in AI-enabled systems for corridor banking, stablecoin settlement and tokenized assets.

Fasset's $68 Million Series C

The latest funding round gives Fasset a private valuation of $1 billion. That places the company in the fintech unicorn category, a term generally used for privately held companies valued at $1 billion or more.

Japan's SBI Group led the Series C round. The investment also builds on an existing relationship between the two companies, including a strategic partnership involving SBI Remit for international money-transfer infrastructure.

The new financing comes only months after Fasset raised $51 million in its Series B round. Combined with the latest financing, the company says it has raised $119 million during 2026.

What Fasset Is Building

Fasset describes itself as a stablecoin-powered neobanking and financial infrastructure platform. Its model is designed to connect users and businesses with financial services across multiple markets while using digital assets and stablecoins as part of the underlying settlement infrastructure.

A major component of the company's strategy is Own Network, which Fasset says connects banks, telecommunications companies, payment providers and liquidity providers across more than 100 international banking corridors.

Stablecoins can be used within this infrastructure as a settlement mechanism, while Fasset is also developing AI-powered systems designed to support corridor banking, transaction routing, stablecoin settlement and tokenized assets.

Why SBI Group's Investment Matters

SBI Group is one of Japan's major financial groups, with businesses spanning financial services, investment and digital assets.

SBI Group has invested across the digital-asset sector, including companies such as Ripple, Circle and Morpho, as well as crypto liquidity provider B2C2.

For Fasset, the relationship could provide access to a broader financial network as the company attempts to expand its cross-border payment and settlement infrastructure across Asia, the Middle East and other emerging markets.

Fasset Reports More Than $40B in Annualized Volume

Fasset says its platform has reached more than $40 billion in annualized transaction volume. The company also reports more than 3 million wallets, over 1,000 enterprise customers and a presence spanning 125 countries.

Those figures illustrate the scale of the network Fasset is building, although they are company-reported operating metrics rather than independently audited financial results.

The company says the Series C capital will help it expand this infrastructure and develop additional payment and financial corridors.

Where the New Capital Will Go

Fasset says the fresh funding will support several areas of its expansion strategy.

  • Own Network expansion: Fasset plans to expand the infrastructure connecting banks, payment providers, liquidity providers and other financial institutions.
  • AI-powered financial systems: The company plans to increase investment in systems supporting corridor banking, stablecoin settlement, transaction routing and tokenized assets.
  • International expansion: Fasset aims to develop additional cross-border financial corridors using strategic partnerships and its growing international network.

Stablecoins Move Beyond Crypto Trading

The Fasset funding round comes at a time when stablecoins are increasingly being explored for uses beyond cryptocurrency trading.

Financial institutions, fintech companies and payment providers are examining stablecoins for cross-border transfers, settlement and other forms of digital money movement.

That broader infrastructure opportunity is where Fasset is attempting to position itself. Rather than treating stablecoins only as crypto assets, the company is building services around their potential use as settlement rails.

The involvement of a major Japanese financial group such as SBI also highlights the increasing overlap between traditional finance and digital-asset infrastructure.

The Road Ahead

Reaching a $1 billion private valuation is a major milestone, but the next stage will be about execution.

Fasset now has additional capital to expand its network, build AI-enabled financial systems and develop more international payment corridors. The challenge will be turning those investments into sustainable growth while operating across different regulatory and financial environments.

SBI Group's involvement could become particularly important as Fasset expands its presence in Asia and other emerging markets.

The company's ability to maintain growth, expand its network and convert transaction activity into durable revenue will ultimately determine whether the latest valuation can be supported over the long term.

The Bottom Line

Fasset has raised $68 million in Series C funding led by Japan's SBI Group, taking its private valuation to $1 billion and moving the company into unicorn territory.

The round follows Fasset's $51 million Series B and brings its total 2026 fundraising to $119 million. The company plans to use the capital to expand Own Network and develop AI-enabled systems for cross-border banking, stablecoin settlement and tokenized assets.

But the bigger story may be the growing institutional interest in the infrastructure surrounding stablecoins.

Stablecoins are increasingly being explored as rails for moving money across borders, and Fasset is positioning its platform around that opportunity. With SBI Group leading the latest funding round, the company now has additional financial and strategic backing to pursue that expansion.

The next question is whether Fasset can turn that growing network into sustainable long-term growth.
