How Supporters Turned the Marmot Project Into a Crypto Fundraising Story

A scientific project studying marmots for more than six decades probably wasn't expected to become a cryptocurrency story. Yet that is exactly what happened in 2026, when an unusual fundraising campaign around Colorado's yellow-bellied marmots caught the attention of an online crypto community.

Supporters independently created a Solana-based meme coin called $ONLYMARMS. The token was not originally launched by the scientists themselves, but its creator fees were directed toward the marmot research effort. Combined with other donations and fundraising, the broader campaign has now raised more than $100,000, according to recent reporting.

The story is unusual, but it also highlights something more serious: the researchers are trying to keep a long-running scientific record alive at a time when reliable research funding has become harder to secure.

What Is the Marmot Research Project?

The Marmot Research Project is a long-term scientific study of yellow-bellied marmots in Colorado. Researchers have been following the animals since 1962, building one of the longest-running datasets on a wild mammal population.

The researchers study subjects including animal behavior, reproduction, survival, body condition and how marmots respond to environmental changes.

Long-term studies like this are valuable because individual years can tell scientists only so much. Decades of continuous observations can reveal changes that are difficult to see in a short research project.

That continuity was put under pressure by funding problems, forcing the team to experiment with unconventional ways of reaching potential supporters.

How Did OnlyMarms Start?

One of the most unusual fundraising ideas was OnlyMarms, a social-media campaign built around the OnlyFans platform.

Despite the name of the platform, the content was not sexual. Instead, the researchers posted photographs and videos of marmots living in their natural environment.

The idea was partly humorous, but the underlying objective was straightforward: attract attention to the research and raise money to keep the project operating.

The campaign initially generated only a relatively small amount compared with the project's longer-term needs. Then crypto supporters discovered the story.

Where Did $ONLYMARMS Come From?

The cryptocurrency was not created by the Marmot Research Project itself.

Outside supporters of the research created a marmot-themed meme coin on the Solana blockchain. The token became known as $ONLYMARMS.

That distinction is important. A cryptocurrency using the name or imagery of a real-world project does not automatically mean that the organization behind that project issued or endorsed it.

In this case, however, the relationship developed further when project co-lead Julien Martin became involved with the creator-fee arrangement.

How Can a Meme Coin Fund Scientific Research?

The basic mechanism is different from a traditional donation.

When people trade a token using a platform that distributes creator fees, those fees can generate revenue. In the $ONLYMARMS arrangement, creator-related fees were directed toward the marmot research effort.

That means the research team does not need the token itself to remain permanently valuable in order for the arrangement to have generated funding. What matters for the fundraising mechanism is the activity that produces the relevant fees.

How Much Money Has the Campaign Raised?

The figures have changed as the campaign developed, which is why it is important to distinguish between different sources of funding.

Earlier reporting in August said the marmot-themed cryptocurrency had generated more than $88,000 in two weeks. Combined with outside donations and other fundraising, the project had already crossed the $100,000 mark.

More recent reporting continues to describe the overall fundraising effort as having brought in more than $100,000, including cryptocurrency, donations and the OnlyMarms campaign.

That is the safer figure to use for the broader campaign because the token's trading activity and fee generation can change over time.

The Researchers Are Still Aiming Much Higher

Even a six-figure fundraising result does not solve the project's long-term funding problem.

Project co-lead Julien Martin has said that reaching approximately $300,000 would be enough to fund a PhD student working on the project for four years.

That target puts the crypto fundraising story into perspective.

More than $100,000 is meaningful money for a scientific project, but it is not the same as having a stable, multi-year funding source.

The researchers therefore continue to face the broader challenge of keeping a decades-long scientific dataset alive.

Fat Marmot Week Took the Story Beyond Crypto

The cryptocurrency was not the only unusual idea to emerge from the marmot project's fundraising push.

In August 2026, the researchers launched the first-ever Fat Marmot Week, a public competition inspired by Alaska's better-known Fat Bear Week.

Eight yellow-bellied marmots competed as they built up body fat before their winter hibernation. Members of the public could vote for their favorite animal.

The event ran from August 24 through August 29 and attracted more than 23,000 votes.

A six-year-old marmot named Dawn won both the mass-gain and popular-vote categories. Dawn reached roughly 6 kilograms (13.2 pounds), which researchers described as exceptionally large for the species.

The contest was entertaining, but the science behind it is serious. Marmots spend the summer building up fat reserves before spending months underground during hibernation. Having enough stored energy can be critical to surviving the winter.

Fat Marmot Week also gave the researchers another way to bring public attention to a scientific project that might otherwise be known mainly inside academic circles.

Why Does Long-Term Marmot Research Matter?

It may be easy to dismiss marmot research as a niche scientific subject. But long-term ecological datasets can provide information about how wildlife responds to environmental changes over time.

Researchers can compare conditions from one decade with observations from another. That can help scientists examine changes in reproduction, survival, body weight, seasonal behavior and population dynamics.

That is particularly relevant as weather patterns and snow conditions change in mountain environments.

A single year of observations cannot provide the same historical context as a dataset stretching back more than 60 years.

Is $ONLYMARMS an Investment?

This is where readers should separate the fundraising story from the investment question.

$ONLYMARMS is a community-created meme coin. Its association with scientific research does not make it a safe investment.

Meme coins can experience extreme price swings, limited liquidity and rapidly changing market interest. A token can fall sharply even while its underlying charitable or community purpose remains unchanged.

In other words, supporting the research and buying the token are not necessarily the same decision.

Someone who wants to support the Marmot Research Project should understand exactly where their money is going rather than assuming that buying a speculative token is equivalent to making a direct donation.

Solana Memecoins Can Move Extremely Fast

The broader Solana memecoin market also helps explain why caution is necessary.

Galaxy Research reported in 2025 that the median token holding time on Solana was roughly 100 seconds, down from about 300 seconds a year earlier. The research described an increasingly fast, speculative trading environment.

That figure is a broader Solana market statistic. It does not mean $ONLYMARMS holders specifically keep their tokens for 100 seconds.

The more useful takeaway is that Solana's memecoin market can be highly short-term and speculative. That makes it especially important to distinguish token trading from the research project's actual fundraising.

Did the Scientists Create the Crypto Token?

No.

The token was created independently by supporters of the project.

That is one of the most important facts in the entire story because it prevents readers from assuming that the Marmot Research Project launched a cryptocurrency as an official financial product.

The later connection between the token's creator-fee mechanism and the research project is what turned an independently created meme coin into an unexpected fundraising channel.

Why the Story Matters for Crypto

Crypto has a long history of tokens built around internet jokes, animals and viral communities.

What makes $ONLYMARMS different is the real-world project sitting behind the meme.

Instead of an animal simply being used as a symbol for speculation, the attention surrounding the marmots was connected to an existing scientific effort.

That does not eliminate the risks associated with the token. But it demonstrates how online communities can sometimes mobilize around causes and turn attention into real-world funding.

Why It Matters for Science

The more important lesson may actually have little to do with cryptocurrency.

Scientific projects that depend on long-term observations need stable funding. A viral campaign can produce a burst of attention, but maintaining research for years requires predictable resources.

The marmot project has shown that researchers can reach audiences far outside traditional scientific institutions.

OnlyMarms, $ONLYMARMS and Fat Marmot Week all attracted people who may never have encountered the project through an academic paper or university website.

That exposure could prove valuable even beyond the money raised.

What Happens Next?

The research team still needs long-term financial support.

The $300,000 target for a four-year PhD position shows that the project requires considerably more than a one-time fundraising boost.

Meanwhile, the researchers have already indicated that Fat Marmot Week will return in 2027, giving the public another opportunity to engage with the animals and the science behind the project.

Whether $ONLYMARMS remains popular in the crypto market is much harder to predict.

Memecoin attention can disappear quickly, and trading activity can change just as fast.

For the scientists, the more durable achievement may be the audience they have built around the research.
