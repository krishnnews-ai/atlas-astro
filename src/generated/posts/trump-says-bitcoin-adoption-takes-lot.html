Trump Says Bitcoin Adoption 'Takes a Lot of Pressure Off Our Dollar,' Calls Crypto a 'Big Deal'

Top Crypto News 2026-08-08T12:58:48.503Z
Bitcoin News

 


Aug 8, 2026 – President Donald Trump has voiced strong support for Bitcoin and the broader cryptocurrency industry, stating that growing adoption of the digital asset is easing pressure on the U.S. dollar and is "a good thing for our country" .

In an interview with Punchbowl News, Trump described cryptocurrency as a "big deal" and highlighted the increasing use of Bitcoin for everyday payments .

"Crypto is a big deal," Trump said. "I see it more and more where people are paying with Bitcoin and they don't even know about cash anymore. That takes a lot of pressure off our dollar. It's a good thing for our country" .

National Security Framing

The president framed his support for crypto as a national security imperative, warning that the United States cannot afford to let China dominate the digital asset space .

"If we don't have it, China's going to have it," Trump said, adding that former President Joe Biden only embraced crypto late because he saw Trump winning the crypto community's support .

Trump placed cryptocurrency alongside artificial intelligence as technologies that could shape global competition, emphasizing that the U.S. should stay ahead as governments compete for influence over digital assets .

Market Reaction: Bitcoin Pushes Past $65,000

The president's comments helped push Bitcoin above $65,000 on Friday, with the asset trading near $65,250 during the session after surging over 4% in the past month .

The move was supported by:

  • Whale accumulation: Wallets holding between 10 and 10,000 BTC increased their balances by 0.34% from July 29 
  • ETF inflows: U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded approximately $754.69 million in net inflows so far in August 
  • Weak jobs data: Nonfarm payrolls fell by 23,000 in July, far below expectations, boosting risk assets 

CLARITY Act Stalls in Senate

Trump's remarks came as lawmakers struggled to advance the CLARITY Act, a bipartisan bill that would establish federal rules for digital asset trading and define oversight roles for regulators .

Senators failed to reach an agreement before the August recess, pushing the next window for action into September .

Trump also addressed proposed ethics rules tied to crypto ownership by federal officials. Some Democrats want tighter restrictions on the president's crypto interests before they support the bill. Trump said he could place assets in a blind trust while arguing against rules that could limit U.S. crypto activity .

Easing Dollar Pressure: A New Narrative

The president's framing of Bitcoin adoption as easing pressure on the dollar represents a shift in the political discourse around digital assets. Rather than viewing crypto as a threat to dollar dominance, Trump positioned organic consumer adoption as a relief valve for the U.S. currency .

This aligns with his broader push to make America "the undisputed Bitcoin superpower and the crypto capital of the world" . His administration has already established a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and a U.S. digital assets stockpile, reversing the previous administration's approach of selling seized crypto assets .

What This Means for Markets

The president's comments reinforce the growing political consensus around crypto in Washington, even as regulatory details remain unresolved. With the CLARITY Act stalled until September, the industry faces continued uncertainty around federal oversight .

Bitcoin's technical positioning near the $66,896 resistance level has traders watching for a potential breakout toward $70,000 . Combined with sustained ETF inflows and whale accumulation, market sentiment remains cautiously optimistic.
