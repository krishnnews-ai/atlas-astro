Bitcoin Needs $95,300 to Flip Meta and Re-enter the Top 10 — Here's How Far It Is

 Cointelegraph shared a fun fact today: Bitcoin needs to hit roughly $95,300 to pass Meta and re-enter the world's top 10 companies.

So where is Bitcoin right now? It has been bouncing between $84,000 and $87,000 for the past few days. That means it needs an $8,000 to $11,000 jump to reach that level. Not a small move.

But this isn't the first time Bitcoin has been in the top 10.

It happened in 2021 and again in 2024-25. Back then, it left companies like Meta and Tesla behind. Its market cap was around $1.3 trillion at the time.

So what's different now?

1. Meta is not the same company anymore. Mark Zuckerberg has poured billions into AI and the metaverse. Meta's market cap is now around $1.5 trillion. Bitcoin doesn't just need a price jump — Meta also needs to slow down.

2. The options market is confused. Deribit data showed max pain at $76,000 for the September expiry, while Bitcoin was trading near $85,000. That tells you traders aren't sure where the next move is going.

3. ETF flows are still positive. Money is still flowing into U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs — slowly but steadily. If that picks up, $95,300 becomes more realistic.

What should you watch?

If Bitcoin holds above $88,000, the next resistance sits around $92,000–$95,000. But if it drops below $84,000, it will likely test $80,000 again — and the top 10 talk fades away.

One thing is clear: Beating Meta is not easy. But in crypto, anything is possible. People said the same thing in 2021.

Bottom Line

Bitcoin needs $95,300 to flip Meta and re-enter the top 10. It's trading around $85,000 right now. The road is long, but not impossible.
