Hunter Biden’s newly launched $LAPTOP memecoin experienced a brutal first trading session, with market data reported by Traders Union showing the token falling approximately 86.5% within 30 minutes of its debut.

The token reportedly dropped from around $199.50 to $26.88 during that initial period, while trading volume exceeded $2.5 million. The sharp move highlights just how quickly political meme coins can move once trading begins.

$LAPTOP is particularly notable because it is tied directly to one of the most politically charged stories involving Hunter Biden: the laptop controversy that emerged during the 2020 U.S. presidential election. The token was launched on Base, the Ethereum layer-2 network associated with Coinbase.

What Is Hunter Biden’s $LAPTOP Memecoin?

$LAPTOP is a political-themed meme cryptocurrency associated with Hunter Biden, the son of former U.S. President Joe Biden.

The name references the laptop that became the subject of intense political controversy after it was reported that Hunter Biden had left a computer at a Delaware repair shop in 2019. The story became a major issue during the 2020 presidential campaign and generated years of political debate.

Rather than avoiding that history, the $LAPTOP project uses the controversy as its central theme.

Hunter Biden publicly teased the token before launch, including an X post featuring media coverage of the laptop story. Multiple outlets reported that the token would launch on Base with a total supply of one billion tokens.

$LAPTOP Falls 86.5% Shortly After Trading Begins

The biggest story surrounding the launch was not the political symbolism. It was the speed of the price collapse.

According to a market report published after the launch, $LAPTOP dropped approximately 86.5% within its first 30 minutes of trading, moving from roughly $199.50 to $26.88.

The same report said the token had already generated more than $2.5 million in trading volume, showing that substantial speculative activity arrived despite the project's extremely short trading history.

A move of that size does not automatically establish that the project was a scam or a rug pull. A newly launched meme token can experience enormous price swings because liquidity is initially limited and traders compete to buy or sell during the opening minutes.

That distinction matters. A crash is evidence of extreme volatility; it is not, by itself, proof of fraud.

Why Did the Price Move So Violently?

There are several structural reasons why a newly launched meme coin can experience a dramatic price move.

1. Very Early Liquidity

New tokens often begin trading with much less liquidity than established cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin or Ethereum. When large orders enter a thin market, prices can move rapidly.

2. Speculative Launch Trading

Some traders buy new meme coins immediately after launch hoping to capture an early price spike. Others sell as soon as momentum weakens.

That creates a market where buying and selling pressure can change within seconds rather than hours.

3. Political Attention

$LAPTOP arrived with an unusually recognizable political name. That gives the token a built-in news cycle and social-media audience, but attention does not necessarily translate into sustainable demand.

4. Meme Coin Economics

Meme coins are generally driven more by community interest, branding, speculation and social momentum than by traditional cash flows or business fundamentals.

That makes their prices particularly sensitive to changes in sentiment.

How Is the $LAPTOP Supply Distributed?

Reports before launch said the project planned a total supply of 1 billion $LAPTOP tokens.

Approximately 30% was allocated to Hunter Biden and the project's founders. Those tokens were reported to be locked for an initial period and then subject to a longer vesting schedule.

Another 20% was designated for distributions involving people who lost money on Donald Trump's $TRUMP meme coin as well as subscribers connected to Hunter Biden's media channels.

The project also described a mechanism under which as much as 30% of the supply could be burned depending on whether specified political and market-related events occur.

If those conditions are not met, reporting indicated that the relevant tokens could instead be directed toward charitable purposes.

The remaining allocation was described as supporting liquidity, charity and the administrative, legal and compliance costs associated with the project.

Why the Trump $TRUMP Connection Matters

One of the more unusual aspects of $LAPTOP is its relationship with traders who lost money on Donald Trump's $TRUMP meme coin.

Reporting before the launch said a portion of the $LAPTOP supply would be distributed to wallets associated with people who suffered losses from $TRUMP.

That makes the token more than a simple celebrity meme coin. Its marketing strategy directly references the political and crypto ecosystem surrounding Trump-era meme coins.

It also gives the project an obvious narrative: a meme coin associated with Hunter Biden attempting to attract attention from traders who previously participated in Trump's crypto venture.

$LAPTOP Is Not the Same as an Investment in Hunter Biden

One important distinction for readers is that buying $LAPTOP does not represent ownership in Hunter Biden, a company controlled by him, or a traditional financial asset.

It is a cryptocurrency token whose value depends on market trading and demand.

There is also no basis for assuming that a politically famous name guarantees long-term value. The initial 86.5% decline demonstrates the opposite: attention can produce enormous trading activity while still being accompanied by equally enormous downside.

Is the $LAPTOP Crash a Rug Pull?

Not based on the price decline alone.

The term “rug pull” generally implies that insiders or developers have deliberately extracted liquidity or otherwise abandoned a token in a way that leaves investors with losses.

A sharp price fall can happen for many reasons, including profit-taking, thin liquidity, panic selling or a collapse in speculative demand.

At the time of this report, the available evidence confirms the severe early price decline, but the decline itself should not be presented as proof that Hunter Biden or the project's founders conducted a rug pull.

Why Political Meme Coins Keep Attracting Attention

Political meme coins have become a recurring part of the crypto market because they combine two powerful internet-native forces: politics and speculation.

Donald Trump and Melania Trump previously launched their own meme coins, and other political figures or personalities have also experimented with crypto assets.

These tokens can generate enormous attention because the underlying brand is already familiar to millions of people.

But familiarity is not the same thing as utility.

That is one of the most important lessons from the $LAPTOP launch. A recognizable name can create liquidity and trading interest, but it cannot guarantee that buyers will continue supporting the token once the initial excitement fades.

What the $LAPTOP Crash Means for Meme Coin Traders

The first lesson is simple: launch-day price action can be extremely misleading.

A token can rise dramatically within minutes and still collapse shortly afterward. The reverse is also possible, but neither direction tells investors what the long-term value of the project will be.

The second lesson is liquidity.

Traders looking at a new token should pay attention not only to the displayed market capitalization but also to actual liquidity, trading volume, holder distribution and the concentration of tokens among large wallets.

The third lesson is supply structure.

Large allocations controlled by founders or insiders can create additional market risks, even when those tokens are subject to lockups or vesting schedules.

Could $LAPTOP Recover?

It is too early to make a reliable prediction.

A meme coin can recover after a sharp decline if a large community forms around it and trading activity remains strong. It can also continue falling if attention disappears.

For $LAPTOP, the market will ultimately determine whether the Hunter Biden connection creates lasting demand or simply produces a short-lived news cycle.

There is no credible basis for assigning a future price target solely from the token's launch performance.

The Bigger Story: Politics Is Becoming Part of Crypto's Speculation Economy

The $LAPTOP launch is another example of how political identity and cryptocurrency speculation are becoming increasingly intertwined.

Unlike traditional political fundraising, a meme coin can trade continuously on blockchain-based markets and attract participants from around the world.

That creates a strange financial environment where political symbolism can become a tradable asset.

But the 86.5% opening collapse also demonstrates the other side of that model. Political attention can bring buyers into a market quickly, but it can disappear just as quickly.
