SwarmBase (SWARM) — Live on Multiple Exchanges

Launched: August 19, 2026

SwarmBase ($SWARM) is now trading on Bitget, KuCoin, MEXC, and PancakeSwap.

What It Is: SwarmBase is an AI infrastructure platform built on BNB Chain for creating and coordinating autonomous AI agent swarms.

Key Details

  • Total Supply: 1 billion SWARM, fixed
  • Community Allocation: 20% of total supply, with 10% unlocked at TGE and 10% linearly vested
  • Funding: Approximately $7 million raised from Castrum Capital, M2M Capital, Notch Ventures, and Becker Ventures
  • Deflationary Mechanism: 20% of platform fees are burned permanently
  • Team/Strategic Partners: 30% allocation with a 12-month cliff lock

Exchange Listings

  • KuCoin: Trading began August 19, 2026, at 10:00 UTC
  • Bitget: Trading began August 19, 2026, at 10:00 UTC
  • Withdrawals: Available from August 20, 2026, on Bitget

Linera (LNRA) — "Next Hyperliquid" Aspiration

Token Sale Announced: August 20, 2026

Linera, founded by former Libra (Meta) team members, has announced an upcoming LNRA token sale and positioned itself as aiming to become the "next Hyperliquid."

What They're Building: Linera is developing a consumer-focused blockchain that includes a real-time PvP prediction market available through its platform.

Core product: Linera Originals is designed around prediction markets that can open, operate, and settle within a short time frame.

Key Features

  • Microchains Parallel Architecture: Supports thousands of smaller markets running simultaneously
  • Finality Under 1 Second: Blocks are designed for sub-second finality
  • Linera Originals: Prediction markets designed to open, operate, and settle within one minute
  • Pure PvP Mode: No house or external market makers
  • Small Team: Approximately five core members

Access: Users who earn in-app badges through product participation will receive priority access to a dedicated allocation pool for the LNRA sale.

Status: Full sale details, including size, pricing, and allocation, have not yet been published and are expected in a separate announcement.

Flop Labs (FLOP) — Arthur Hayes' Return

Status: Airdrop Planned Q4 2026, Genesis Block Targeted Q1 2027

Arthur Hayes, co-founder of BitMEX, has announced his return to the crypto industry through Flop Labs.

What It Is: Flop Labs is proposing a network where AI agents can use FLOP to pay for computing power, inference services, and storage. GPU providers would supply resources, while validators would confirm service delivery.

Key Commitments

  • No Presale
  • No Venture-Capital Allocation
  • Fair Launch: The project has described the planned distribution as a "100% fair launch"

Timeline

  • Q4 2026: FLOP airdrop planned
  • Q1 2027: Flop Network genesis block targeted

Important Note: Flop Labs has not yet published a token allocation schedule, airdrop rules, a claim process, or complete technical documentation explaining how FLOP will function before genesis.

The planned token distribution would therefore occur before the network supporting its intended utility is live. Investors and users should treat the project as early-stage and verify official documentation as it becomes available.

What to Watch Next

These four projects are at very different stages. SwarmBase is already trading across multiple venues, while Linera is moving toward its token sale. Flop Labs remains in an early development phase, and ATA Chain is preparing for its scheduled exchange listing.

For anyone following new crypto launches, the important distinction is between a token that is already trading and a project that is still preparing its network or distribution. Launch dates, token allocations, exchange schedules, and technical plans can change, so checking the latest official announcements before making any decision remains important.
