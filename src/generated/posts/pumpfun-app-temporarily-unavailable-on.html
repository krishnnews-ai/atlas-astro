Pump.fun App Temporarily Unavailable on Apple’s US and India App Stores

Top Crypto News 2026-09-11T12:33:23.166Z
Market NewsTrending NewsAltcoin News



By CoinAINews Staff

Pump.fun’s mobile app is temporarily unavailable for new downloads from Apple’s App Store in the United States and India, according to a latest update from the Pump.fun mobile team.

The development affects users who are trying to install the Pump.fun app for the first time on iPhone or iPad in the two markets. People who already have the app installed are reportedly unaffected and can continue using it as usual.

The app also remains available to Android users through Google Play, meaning the current issue is not a complete shutdown of Pump.fun’s mobile service.

What Happened to the Pump.fun App?

Pump.fun’s mobile team said the application is temporarily unavailable to download from the U.S. and India iOS App Stores.

The update specifically refers to new downloads. Existing users who already have the application installed can continue using it normally, while new users on Android can download the application through Google Play.

The timing of the restriction has attracted attention because Pump.fun is one of the best-known platforms for launching and trading memecoins on Solana. Its official website continues to promote the mobile experience and describes the platform as a place to discover and trade internet trends and tokens.

Existing iOS Users Are Not Affected

For users who already installed Pump.fun on an iPhone or iPad, the latest update does not indicate a service shutdown.

The Pump.fun mobile team said existing users can continue using the app normally. It also specifically stated that users’ funds are safe.

That makes the current development different from a full suspension of the Pump.fun application. The immediate problem is focused on new iOS downloads in the United States and India.

Android Users Can Still Download Pump.fun

The Android version remains available through Google Play, according to the latest update from the Pump.fun mobile team.

This means users who are new to Pump.fun and use Android still have an official mobile download option, while new iOS users in the affected regions currently have to wait for availability to return.

Why Is Pump.fun Temporarily Unavailable on Apple?

The exact reason for the temporary iOS download restriction has not been publicly confirmed.

That is an important distinction. There is currently no verified information establishing that Apple has permanently banned Pump.fun, that regulators ordered its removal, or that the restriction was caused by a particular compliance or policy issue.

Until Pump.fun or Apple provides a specific explanation, those claims would be speculation.

The Pump.fun mobile team has described the situation as temporary, but a specific restoration time was not provided in the update reviewed for this report.

Is Pump.fun Still Operating?

Yes. The current development does not indicate that Pump.fun has shut down.

The Pump.fun website remains operational, and its official platform continues to offer token creation, discovery and trading functionality. The website also continues to advertise its mobile experience.

The company's published terms describe Pump as a platform designed to help users create and trade digital assets.

What Does the App Store Issue Mean for New Users?

The immediate impact is mainly about access.

A new iPhone user in the United States or India who wants to install Pump.fun may currently be unable to obtain the application through Apple's App Store. Existing users, however, are not facing the same reported restriction.

Android users remain able to access the application through Google Play, while the web version of Pump.fun remains available.

For a platform that relies heavily on fast-moving memecoin activity and mobile users, even a temporary regional download interruption can create friction for onboarding new users.

Pump.fun's Mobile Push Comes Under the Spotlight

The incident also highlights how dependent crypto applications can be on the distribution policies of major mobile platforms.

Pump.fun has increasingly positioned its mobile product as an important part of its trading experience. Its website describes mobile access as a faster way to interact with the platform, while its help center includes dedicated guides covering mobile trading, deposits, withdrawals, coin creation and wallet functions.

That makes App Store availability an important part of the platform's ability to reach new users.
