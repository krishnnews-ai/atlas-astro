🔍 What Is the CLARITY Act?

The CLARITY Act (officially H.R. 3633) is the most advanced crypto market structure bill in U.S. history . It would:

  • Divide oversight of digital assets between the SEC and CFTC 

  • Answer the classification question that determines everything downstream 

  • Provide legal certainty for the entire crypto industry

Key Milestones:

  • ✅ Passed the House: 294-134 (July 2025) 

  • ✅ Passed Senate Banking Committee: 15-9 (May 2026) 

  • ✅ Placed on Senate Calendar: June 1, 2026 

  • ❌ Not yet scheduled for floor vote 

⚠️ Why Is It Stuck?

1. The 60-Vote Filibuster Hurdle

Republicans hold 53 Senate seats, meaning they need at least 7 Democratic votes to clear the 60-vote cloture threshold .

"Democrats won't be moved by crypto cash at this point." — Punchbowl News reporter Brendan Pedersen 

2. Democratic Demands

Senate Democrats are conditioning their support on :

  • Stricter ethics language

  • Illicit-finance provisions

  • Stablecoin yield regulations

3. Senate Leadership Priorities

Majority Leader John Thune admitted on July 23 that he did not expect the bill to pass before recess, saying: "I would like to at least get Clarity started. We'll see where the votes are" .

🔮 What Happens If It Fails?

1. No Second Chance in 2026

The Senate's tentative calendar lists August 10 through September 11 as a state work period . Senators return on September 14, but:

  • November midterms are approaching

  • The legislative calendar is crowded

  • A delay into 2027 risks restarting the framework before a Congress whose composition is unknown 

2. The "Walking Dead" Scenario

Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan described the potential limbo as the industry entering a "walking dead" period — neither dead nor alive, just stuck .

3. Senator Lummis Warns of 2030

Senator Cynthia Lummis has warned that market structure legislation could slip to 2030 or die entirely at the end of the 119th Congress in January 2027, forcing a full restart .
