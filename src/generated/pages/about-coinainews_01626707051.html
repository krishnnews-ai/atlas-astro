About CoinAINews

CoinAINews is a crypto and AI news publication built for people who want to understand what's actually happening in digital assets — without the hype, jargon, or noise.

We started this site because crypto news had become either too technical for regular readers or too sensational to be useful. There was a gap: coverage that treats you like an intelligent adult, explains things clearly, and doesn't try to sell you anything.

That's what we try to do every day.

What We Cover

We publish daily coverage of:

  • Bitcoin, Ethereum, and major altcoins
  • Blockchain technology and infrastructure
  • Artificial intelligence and its intersection with crypto
  • DeFi, stablecoins, and Web3
  • Crypto regulation in India, the US, EU, and beyond
  • Exchange updates, security incidents, and market trends
  • NFTs and emerging technologies

Whether you're an investor, a developer, a trader, or just someone curious about where this technology is going — we write for you.

How We Work

Our editorial team is small and based in India. We publish stories based on publicly available information, official announcements, on-chain data, industry reports, and credible news sources.

We don't take payment for coverage. We don't publish sponsored content without clearly labeling it. If we get something wrong, we correct it and note the change.

We're not perfect. But we try to be honest, transparent, and useful.

Editorial Standards

Every article we publish goes through a simple process:

  • We check facts against primary sources wherever possible
  • We distinguish between news, analysis, and opinion
  • We update articles when new information changes the story
  • We correct errors publicly when they're significant

If you spot a mistake, tell us. We'd rather fix it than pretend it didn't happen.

What We're Not

We're not financial advisors. Nothing on CoinAINews is investment, legal, or tax advice. Crypto markets are volatile, and you should do your own research before making any financial decision.

We're also not a PR wire. If you send us a press release, we'll review it — but sending one doesn't guarantee publication. We publish what's relevant to our readers, not what's convenient.

Who's Behind CoinAINews

CoinAINews is run by a small editorial team based in India. We're a group of writers and analysts who've been following crypto and AI for years. We're not backed by a venture fund. We're not owned by an exchange. We're independent.
